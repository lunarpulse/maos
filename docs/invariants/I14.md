## Routed FR53 witness (20-3b AC5, green at 20-3c)

`crates/maos-kernel-core/tests/routed_halt_swap_20_3b.rs` exercises the real
coordinator with an active halt, pins the original halt identity and full replay
metadata, and verifies the predecessor snapshot reaches the successor. A hot
swap keeps the pid, so since 20-3c (K1p, D-20-3c-01 A) the continuity gate
**carries** the predecessor's pending halts instead of draining them
(`validate_swap_halt_continuity`; `HotSwapPrecheck::check` judges the same set),
and the witness is green. `halt_carry_20_3c.rs` pins the rest of the contract:
another Spirit's halt is never read, an incompatible successor refuses the swap
with the halt intact, a swap compensated after the gate leaves the halt pending,
and the carried halt resolves through the unchanged resolver path. This
invariant remains **asserted**, not guaranteed: there is no property test and
adversarial corpus pair, and the spawned-form swap is owned by
`20-9-spawned-form-lifecycle-hooks`, which must inherit carry rather than
re-add a drain. Do not weaken the witness to successor re-firing.
