## Routed FR53 witness (20-3b AC5, green at 20-3c)

`crates/maos-kernel-core/tests/routed_halt_swap_20_3b.rs` exercises the real
coordinator with an active halt, pins the original halt identity and full replay
metadata, and verifies the predecessor snapshot reaches the successor. A hot
swap keeps the pid, so since 20-3c (K1p, D-20-3c-01 A) the continuity gate
**carries** the predecessor's pending halts instead of draining them
(`validate_swap_halt_continuity`; `HotSwapPrecheck::check` judges the same set),
and the witness is green. `halt_carry_20_3c.rs` pins the rest of the contract:
another Spirit's halt is never read, an incompatible successor refuses the swap
with the halt intact, a swap compensated after the gate leaves the halt pending,
and the carried halt resolves through the unchanged resolver path. This
invariant is **asserted**, not guaranteed: the 21-10 property test and
adversarial corpus pair is a non-claiming witness (see the downgrade note under Predicate), and the spawned-form swap is owned by
`20-9-spawned-form-lifecycle-hooks`, which must inherit carry rather than
re-add a drain. Do not weaken the witness to successor re-firing.

## Predicate (21-10)

**Downgraded to `asserted` (2026-11-22 independent review F3/F6).** Witness strengthening owed; production cutover race open (owner: 21-9 hot-swap). The hot-swap coordinator accepts an undeclared successor when the carried snapshot is empty and then awaits hooks before replacing the runtime, so a halt registered in that window crosses; the 21-10 witness drives the swap gate and the standalone precheck (typed refusal asserted) but cannot fail on that cutover race. A row is `guaranteed` only if its witness fails without enforcement; this one does not yet.

Known residual, not claimed: a halt registered between the gate snapshot and the cutover can cross to a successor that declares no compatibility. For the halts the gate judged (fix at origin: a swap, and `HotSwapCoordinator::precheck`, refuse an undeclared or mismatched successor), a swap with a non-empty halt set carries every halt with its resolution state (or drains all with receipts) and needs a successor declaring a matching `halt_protocol_compatibility`; spawned vectors: `spawned_swap_20_9::a_hot_swap_keeps_the_pid_tokens_memory_and_state_and_waits_for_the_active_turn` and `::a_predecessor_that_exits_after_its_snapshot_still_completes_its_swap`. Callable as `maos_domain::invariants::i14::check`.
