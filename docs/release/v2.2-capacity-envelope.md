## The blast axis — a measured breach, not a met floor

`max_blast_radius` in the table below is **DERIVED + REPORTED, never a met
floor.** Story 14-1's ratified concurrent-adversary race measured NFR-Rel-7's
`blast radius ≤ 5 peers` **BREACHED at both scales — 6–8 at N=100, 7 at N=30**,
scale-independent. The cause is a requirement defect, fixed at source rather
than tuned: the floor was written with no adversary concurrency model, and it
cannot coexist with NFR-Rel-9's measured revocation propagation
(p50 ≤ 30 s / p99 ≤ 90 s, asserted by `t_10_4b_rotation_real_timing.rs:338`) — holding a concurrent adversary to five peers would
need isolation to complete inside ~12 ms on loopback. See the
`[DELTA-2026-08-28]` amendment on NFR-Rel-7
([`prd/non-functional-requirements.md:26`](../../_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prd/non-functional-requirements.md))
and `RELEASE-HOLDS.md` row 18.

What this release may state on that axis is the mechanism-derived property the
gate actually binds: **detection interrupts propagation**, via two conjuncts —
reach `≤ 4W` (`W = RACE_IN_FLIGHT = 6`), a constant that cannot grow with the
fleet and is sized to catch a *structural* failure to interrupt rather than a
merely slow detector (slowness binds on the detection-latency axis); and
`reach × 2 < offered`, derived from the run itself so it reds at every scale.
Proven falsifiable live: the no-detection `SweepOnly` leg reds both (99 > 24,
99 × 2 ≥ 99) and the over-reach leg reds the absolute predicate. Measured clean
reach is 6–8 isolated and 10 under concurrent load, against the 24 constant. `≤5` is retained unchanged as the
**serial-adversary** floor and stays unproven for a concurrent adversary.

## The axis, stated explicitly

NFR-Scale-5 names the **host / institution** axis; those terms remain
noninterchangeable. Reza is **one institution with three teams**, while the
live [`cortex_fourteen_institution_isolation_live`](../../crates/maos-bin/tests/cross_team_crossing_13_6b.rs)
leg provisions **fourteen independent institutions**. Each institution has its
own authority key and pinned verification state, signed manifest, identity and
host→team binding, plus a distinct team datname on the real Postgres substrate.

The fourteen-institution evidence proves the institution boundary—not a
renamed host count: its authority witnesses reconcile as an exact unique set;
a foreign manifest is refused by the crossing applier with typed
`ConsentDenied`; removing one institution leaves the other thirteen authority
pins and no-grant consent decisions intact; and a signed donor-authority clone
is rejected against the recipient's original pin. Host, team, and institution
are therefore still three separate axes. The N=30 table below remains **host
churn** evidence, not a fourteen-institution performance measurement.

## Measurement limits — where the teeth actually are

**N=30 is a host count**, specifically live `TcpA2ATransport` endpoints bound
on loopback; it is not a count of institutions or teams. [`t_11_3_scale_churn.rs:169-180`](../../crates/maos-a2a-tcp/tests/t_11_3_scale_churn.rs)
Those endpoint accept loops are in-process `tokio::spawn` tasks
([`transport.rs:343-365`](../../crates/maos-a2a-tcp/src/transport.rs)), in one
OS test process on a co-located `127.0.0.1` mTLS mesh.
[`t_11_3_scale_churn.rs:1-7`](../../crates/maos-a2a-tcp/tests/t_11_3_scale_churn.rs)

Reachability is deliberately hub-and-spoke: **2 × (N − 1) = 58** dials at
**N = 30**, not a full **N × (N − 1) = 870** directed non-self dial sweep. The stable hub
shows the mesh is not partitioned after each round; it does not prove every
non-hub pair was dialed. [`t_11_3_scale_churn.rs:40-56`](../../crates/maos-a2a-tcp/tests/t_11_3_scale_churn.rs)
The recorded **0.79 s** live drill is consequently well under one second.
[`11-3-scale-envelope-25-30-host-churn.md:303`](../../_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/11-3-scale-envelope-25-30-host-churn.md)

The clean loopback pass clears the **1-hour / 5-peer / 24-hour / 4-hour** floors
in three to four orders of magnitude. That is not a measurement of capacity.
The evidence with teeth is the falsifier suite and real reachability outcomes:

> “The clean loopback pass is trivial by design … The gate's teeth are therefore
> FALSIFIERS — and every falsifier here is driven by a REAL reachability outcome
> on the live mesh, never a hand-injected constant into `from_real_events`.”
> [`t_11_3_scale_churn.rs:14-17`](../../crates/maos-a2a-tcp/tests/t_11_3_scale_churn.rs)

The churn report makes the loopback limit explicit:

> “`rto_secs` is DERIVED + REPORTED + advisory-if-breached (own falsifier),
> NOT a v2.0 ship-block — a real >4h RTO breach is physically unobservable on
> the co-located loopback mesh this drill runs on (L5). `rto_secs` PROMOTES to
> a binding floor at v2.5 once real geo-distributed hosts make a breach
> observable.”
> [`crates/maos-a2a-core/src/chaos/churn.rs:14-17`](../../crates/maos-a2a-core/src/chaos/churn.rs)

## Supersession

This document supersedes **only the 25-host churn half** of the historical
statement at
[`docs/release/v1.5-topology-support.md:8`](v1.5-topology-support.md).
Story 11.3 replaced the synthetic 3-host scaffold with a live 30-host
regression drill, and `check-scale-churn` controls that measured host-axis
envelope.

The separate **14-institution Cortex isolation target is now established only
for authority-pin binding, cross-institution crossing refusal, and independent
removal/revocation behavior** by
`cortex_fourteen_institution_isolation_live`. It does **not** establish
performance or throughput SLOs at fourteen institutions, a 30-day soak,
geo-distribution or geo latency, or a full N×N mesh. The host-churn envelope —
the N=30 compressed scale plus the N=100 envelope Story 14-1 landed — and the
fourteen-institution isolation evidence remain distinct measurements.
