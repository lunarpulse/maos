# Code of Conduct

MAOS follows the Contributor Covenant v2.1.

> **Full document:** [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](https://github.com/lunarpulse/maos/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Summary

We are committed to making participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute
to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to **maos-conduct@lunarpulse.dev**.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
