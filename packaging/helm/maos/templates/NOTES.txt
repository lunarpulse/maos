  kubectl exec -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "maos.fullname" . }}-0 -c maos -- /usr/local/bin/maosctl status
  kubectl exec -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "maos.fullname" . }}-0 -c maos -- /usr/local/bin/maosctl agents

The operator door listens on loopback inside the pod only; this chart renders no Service for it.
The home is the PersistentVolumeClaim "home-{{ include "maos.fullname" . }}-0" at /var/lib/maos.

NOT SUPPORTED: T3 Workers. They need a rootless container runtime, which a pod does not provide
by default. This chart mounts no container socket and requests no privilege to work around that.
