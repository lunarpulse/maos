## Authoritative completion boundary (Main ruling, 2026-11-05)

The governing boundary is a **durable transactional linearization marker**, not handler return or client receipt time. Completion means that, at a single TL writer transaction which re-checks high-water and records completion, no matching row committed before that marker survives. Rows committed after the marker are post-completion data and are not claimed erased. If the marker cannot be established within the finite pass cap, report pending/retention, never complete. No writer-barrier work belongs in L1. Independent same-UID writers without barriers require a named limitation and rule-10 disposition in the maintained records.

## Frozen-kernel coverage boundary

Read-only inspection of `memory/mod.rs:521-570` shows the kernel checks the hold once, calls the IAC scrub, then deletes private namespaces and principal-index rows without a shared hold lease. IAC recheck/body-mutation and hold release can be corrected kernel-free; private/index deletion atomicity with a newly placed hold requires the final kernel landing. That remainder must be explicitly priced/dispositioned and must not be reported as closed by this correction.

**Future lease planning price — not a measurement or grant.** [INFERENCE] A kernel call-site/typed-held-result protocol is approximately +4–20 tokei; a shared-authority IAC lease and cross-store lock ordering approximately +30–80. These are unmeasured design forecasts, not a complete implemented prototype. The final kernel preflight must measure the complete protocol and tests, including a hold between the current check and private/index deletion, before requesting a separate grant. At the upper kernel forecast, KS/O4's measured 21060 would become 21080, beyond its existing 21078 ceiling: STOP/re-rule rather than consume the old allowance silently. The whole-ledger 21250 STOP and unchanged L1 kernel pins still apply.

Main approved replacing the IAC inner lock with the already-pinned `parking_lot::FairMutex`, including four mechanical unit-test callers (three poison-result consumers and the fresh-lockability check) in the moved unit module. Test names/assertions must remain unchanged and actual nextest name lists must be re-compared. No sleeps, route/observation-deadline changes or wall/VM threshold changes are authorized. `parking_lot` already exists in `maos-iac/Cargo.toml:17`, with Cargo.lock version 0.12.5; `cargo deny` remains required. The non-poisoning semantic change must be disclosed: the adapter contains no `PoisonError`, `is_poisoned` or panic-catching recovery path; synchronous I2 write-error panics and SQLite transaction rollback remain the fail-closed mechanisms, not a poisoned-state recovery branch.

**Shared-host wall disposition — Main, 2026-11-05.** AC5/EC-13 stays open under 20-11 until hosted proof shows the unchanged <25% ratio and real 1M completion. 20-7 owns its stable-runner timing/I/O half. The temporary diagnostic is not verification: twelve >100 ms holds (eleven during the first operation, one during its repeat) were ordinary-append holds; the scanner waited for them, with no >100 ms scan hold recorded. These are safety repairs, not an append-performance workaround.
