# Security review — commit f56959c2 (story 20-9, correction round 3)

Reviewed the r3 mechanisms as they exist at HEAD (no git/bash tool available in this session, so the review is of the tracked working tree at `f56959c2`, cross-checked against the r2/r3 review texts and tests under `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/20-9-spawned-form-lifecycle-hooks-evidence/`): `crates/maos-bin/src/{spawned_lifecycle.rs, hook_pump.rs, admission.rs, spirit_session.rs, supervision.rs, operator_door.rs}`, `crates/maos-kernel-core/src/hot_swap/{coordinator.rs, migrator.rs, archive.rs}`, `crates/maos-kernel-core/src/scheduler/hook_dispatch.rs`, `crates/maos-manifest/src/manifest.rs`, `crates/maos-control/src/lib.rs`, tests `spawned_swap_20_9.rs`, `spawned_door_20_9.rs`. 14 files, ~9 drivers traced end-to-end.

## Findings (6: 1 High, 2 Medium, 1 Low, 2 Info)

**F1 — HIGH — Swap stages are bounded only by the successor's own declared budget; a silent malicious successor fences the predecessor and holds the daemon-wide upgrade lock for up to ~24 h per stage, and the fence-releasing `Drop` cannot run while the inline exchange is stuck.**
- Where: `crates/maos-bin/src/spawned_lifecycle.rs:117-160` (`Prewarm::exchange`/`next_stdout` wait to `cap`), `:695-712` (`await_ready`/Probe under `launch.turn_budget`), `:230-246` (inline exchange holds the `prewarm` mutex guard for the whole exchange), `:440-452` (fence released only in `Drop`); `crates/maos-bin/src/spirit_session.rs:186-192` (`turn_budget` = manifest `[budget].time_cap_seconds`); `crates/maos-manifest/src/manifest.rs:1184-1189` (cap allowed up to 86 400 s); `crates/maos-kernel-core/src/hot_swap/migrator.rs:78-90` + `scheduler/hook_dispatch.rs:357-364,446-464` (dispatcher timeout = the successor's declared cap, no host wall); `crates/maos-bin/src/operator_door.rs:1408-1409` (one global `upgrade_lock`).
- Exploit: a successor artifact (vetting checks manifest/attestation, not guest behavior) declares `time_cap_seconds = 86400` and simply stays silent. Pre-warm (`await_ready`, Probe) stalls up to 24 h each *while already holding the global `upgrade_lock`* — every tenant's `spirit upgrade` / cold swap / restore is refused-blocked for that time; the door command's route-budget expiry cannot reclaim it, because the factory runs synchronously under `block_in_place` (`main.rs:3375-3380`) and the lock guard lives in that task until the blocking chain returns. After the snapshot reply fences the predecessor (`spirit_session.rs:676-677`), a silent `migrate`/`swap-in` keeps it fenced (no turns, no triggers, mailbox backpressure) for another up-to-24 h per stage. The dispatcher's `timeout` does not abort the `spawn_blocking` exchange, and the successor link is pinned by that closure (`migrator.rs` temp SCB / replaced SCB runtime), so the compensating drop defers `Drop` — the unfence — behind the running exchange guard until the successor's own cap expires. The r3 pump work added exactly this class of host wall (`TELEMETRY_WALL`/`CONSOLIDATE_WALL`, "a subscriber cannot stall either for its whole manifest budget") but the swap exchanges and pre-warm got none.
- Fix sketch: give pre-warm and every swap-stage exchange a host-side wall (`min(declared, PREWARM_WALL≈60 s)`, mirroring `fire_walled`); have the coordinator's compensation release the fence directly (force-kill the pre-warm child / `set_fenced(false)` on `BudgetExceeded`) instead of relying solely on `Drop`; consider a per-spirit (not global) upgrade lock so one stalled swap cannot wedge every tenant's upgrades.

**F2 — MEDIUM — The predecessor session's teardown is unsynchronized with the coordinator's post-`replace_runtime` steps: a clean guest exit in the window panics via `expect` or completes a swap whose successor is never adopted.**
- Where: `crates/maos-kernel-core/src/hot_swap/coordinator.rs:304-328` (step 8 replaces the SCB runtime, then `map.get(&predecessor_pid).expect("successor SCB just inserted")` at `:326`); `crates/maos-bin/src/spirit_session.rs:507-541` (adoption happens only if the session loop is still alive) and `:713-748` (clean exit `return Ok(())` → teardown → `finish`); `crates/maos-bin/src/supervision.rs:393, 1073-1088` (`UnloadClean` disposition + `exec.unload(pid)`); `crates/maos-bin/src/spawned_lifecycle.rs:349-363` (`commit` places the `Handover` on the predecessor link).
- Exploit: the predecessor guest fully controls its own exit timing. Answer `snapshot` (fencing itself), then `exit(0)`. The session's `UnloadClean` unloads the pid while the coordinator is between step 8 and step 9: the `expect` at `coordinator.rs:326` panics the upgrade task (mid-swap state: runtime already replaced, no swap-in fired). If the unload lands between step 9 and step 12, the swap reports `Completed` and the spirit then vanishes (SCB unloaded) with a monitor over a dead pid; if it lands between commit and adoption, the `Handover` (live successor child) is taken by nobody and dies only when the dropped predecessor core releases it. All outcomes are confined to the target spirit (guest pair is the tenant's own), but a task panic and a "completed" upgrade that immediately disappears are operator-visible integrity failures.
- Fix sketch: replace the `expect` with a `NotLoaded`-style error plus compensation; take a swap-in-flight marker/ref on the binding (e.g. `expect_session`-style flag the session teardown honors, or hold the supervisor's binding across steps 8-12) so a session cannot `UnloadClean` a pid whose runtime was just handed a successor; on teardown-before-adoption, take and kill the `Handover` explicitly.

**F3 — MEDIUM — FR11 archive-after-readiness is not crash-safe in either direction: a death between probe and archive admits a later cross-major *downgrade* with no reverse migrator, and a death mid-write leaves a corrupt record that bricks future loads.**
- Where: `crates/maos-bin/src/spawned_lifecycle.rs:596-600` (record written one poll tick after the probe answer, no journaling); `crates/maos-bin/src/admission.rs:1172-1175` (unarchived contract), `:1487-1556` (`refuse_without_migrator` ignores same-version records and picks the newest *other* version), `:1558-1580` (`archive_admitted` is write-if-absent and warn-only on failure); `crates/maos-kernel-core/src/hot_swap/archive.rs:81-82` (plain `fs::write`, no tmp+rename, no fsync).
- Impact: (i) v2.0.0 (cross-major, forward migrator declared) starts, runs turns, possibly writes its schema-2 stores, and the daemon dies (OOM/SIGKILL/power) in the readiness→archive window → no v2 record → a later load of v1.0.0 is judged only against v1's record and admitted with no `v2→v1` migrator — the fail-open downgrade the gate exists to prevent (state in the Spirit's own stores is then read by the older major). (ii) Crash mid-`fs::write` of `manifest.toml` leaves a partial record that write-if-absent never repairs and the fail-closed parser (`ManifestUnreadable/ManifestParse` naming the record) turns into a permanent refusal of every future cross-major load of that spirit until manual repair. The r3 move (record only after readiness) correctly fixed the never-started case; the crash window it opened is unbudgeted.
- Fix sketch: write the record atomically (tmp+rename+fsync of file and dir) and make the durable ordering two-sided — e.g. journal the intent in the Transparency Log before `start` (the `admitted` declaration row already exists) and have `refuse_without_migrator` fall back to it, or write a provisional record before start and remove it in `unwind_admitted`, choosing fail-closed (spurious refusal) over fail-open (unrecorded cross-major run).

**F4 — LOW — Queued-then-started telemetry triggers are billed to the hook: the wall covers queue wait + hook run, so one delivery can quarantine a legitimate busy component.**
- Where: `crates/maos-bin/src/hook_pump.rs:1345-1385` (`fire_walled`'s `timeout(wall)` wraps the whole dispatch, including the session queue wait) and `:446-450` (`BudgetExceeded` quarantines immediately, not 3-of-5); `crates/maos-bin/src/spawned_lifecycle.rs:267-277` (`queue_share = min(cap, TELEMETRY_WALL)/2`) and `:500-527` (a trigger queued behind an active turn that still starts before its give-up deadline).
- Impact: the r3 busy counter only classifies *dropped* triggers; a trigger that queues ≤2.5 s behind the component's own frame turn and then runs, say, 2.5 s exceeds the 4.75 s wall (30 s cap) and quarantines the subscriber for "exceeding its budget" it never exceeded — contradicting the documented "the pump never charges a subscriber for time it spent queued". Same-tenant only (own session), but it converts ordinary busyness into immediate telemetry quarantine.
- Fix sketch: measure the hook from `CallState::start()` (have the link report queue-wait ns, subtract it from the wall the pump enforces, or start `fire_walled`'s clock only once the session claims the call).

**F5 — INFO — `BUSY_DROPS` grows without bound.**
- `crates/maos-bin/src/spawned_lifecycle.rs:428-438`: global `BTreeMap<u32,u64>`, insert-only, pids monotonic and never reused, no pruning on unload. Each distinct pid ever busy-dropped leaks an entry for the daemon's lifetime; an actor able to repeatedly admit churny components grows it without bound (slow, small entries). Fix: clear the entry in `unwind_admitted`/session finalize, or key it by live pid registry.

**F6 — INFO — `redistill_results` slots leak for undelivered forget notices whose writer never reports.**
- `crates/maos-bin/src/hook_pump.rs:233, 880-897`: when a notice is not `completed`, the slot is left with `waiting=false`; it is removed only when the sink eventually fires (`:612-631`). A writer that was unloaded or died before redistilling leaves the slot forever; repeated forgets accumulate. Fix: sweep slots at unregister/shutdown or age them out.

## Rejected candidates (investigated, not exploitable as framed)

1. **Overlapping swaps clobber `predecessor.attempt`** (`attach_successor` overwrites the slot; an older successor's `Drop` would kill the newer attempt and unfence mid-snapshot): all production paths serialize — the door takes the global `upgrade_lock` (`operator_door.rs:1408-1409`) *and* the per-spirit lock (`:2557-2571`; `Upgrade`/`Load` both carry `spirit_key`, `maos-control/src/lib.rs:426-436`); `initiate_swap` has no other production caller. Hardening note only: make `attach_successor` refuse a non-empty attempt.
2. **Cross-tenant wrongful quarantine via flood**: strikes/slow-history are per-subscriber (`hook_pump.rs:382-455`) and measure only that subscriber's own hook execution; busy drops are excluded by the per-pid counter (`:1360-1385`); `sync()` charges only the same runtime object. No path charges tenant A for tenant B's flood. The real cross-tenant effect is the acknowledged R3/R4 residual "global pacing floor": `delivery_loop` (`:1196-1230`) processes events sequentially and joins every per-event task, so one slow-under-wall subscriber paces all deliveries until the broadcast channel lags and events are skipped for everyone — telemetry loss, not quarantine.
3. **Privacy-notice quarantine bypass abuse**: the bypass is deliberate and bounded (`hook_pump.rs:804-807`); a quarantined/withholding subscriber cannot reorder or accelerate erasure — notices precede `certify_erasure` in every sweep iteration (`:860-1000`), undelivered pids are journaled (`forget.purge.undelivered`) and reported, and late redistills are re-validated and scrubbed via `check_redistilled` (`:632-689`).
4. **JoinSet task explosion**: `deliver()` spawns ≤ eligible subscribers per event, sequentially per event; `complete_forget` ≤ registered pids × (1 + ≤8 iterations); the watchdog's passes are `inflight`-guarded per pid (`:1258-1275`). Registry size is bounded by live admissions with lazy `unloaded` cleanup. No unbounded fan-out.
5. **`unwind_admitted` incompleteness**: r3 unregisters the pump with the revoke row (witnessed by `spawned_swap_20_9.rs:794-815`); tokens/capabilities/context-budget are cleaned by session finalize (`spirit_session.rs:766-773`); `rollback` unloads the SCB; promotion set and pid-map are handled in both branches (`admission.rs:1452-1471`). Residual (acknowledged R6, INFO): `authorize_principal_writes` is insert-only in the kernel — no escalation, pids never reused. Footgun note: `remove_pid_by_spirit_id` (`:1593-1600`) removes by key unconditionally — safe only because every caller holds the per-spirit door lock.
6. **Malicious re-fence after a refused swap**: `CallState` (`spawned_lifecycle.rs:36-77`) plus the attempt-flag/fence under the same predecessor mutex (`:385-393`, `:440-452`) make a late snapshot reply fence nothing; covered by `spawned_swap_20_9.rs:517` and `:732`.
7. **`unwrap`/`expect` after serde**: none in scope — admission's toml/serde paths are all `map_err`'d (`:637-1003`, `:1528-1532`), pump payload encodes matched (`:468-475`, `:736-740`), the forget replay decode is `map_err`'d (`operator_door.rs:2256-2259`), `main.rs:9068/9137` are allowlisted infallible `json!` serializations, and `hook_pump.rs:629`'s `expect` is a same-lock invariant, not serde. The coordinator's `expect`s are lock/invariant panics — the reachable one is F2.
8. **Cold-swap unwind racing a concurrent re-admission of the same id**: serialized by the per-spirit lock shared by `Load`/`Upgrade` (see #1).
