## 3. Why it closed early

The full review of 2026-09-04 (*MAOS Promise Ledger*; evidence in `planning-artifacts/review-2026-09-04/`) measured the tree rather than the tracker:

- 22 of 66 FRs live from a product binary; 0 release tags; 1 of 11 Spirits calls the Inference Port; every reference Spirit implements 1 of 14 hooks.
- The discipline `aggregate` job is red at HEAD on four gates plus one deterministic test red from a `done` story.
- Of Epic 14's 11 remaining rows, 9 were gates, registries, ledgers or ceilings. Finishing them was priced at 4–6 weeks with no operator-visible behavior at the end, and 14-7/14-8/14-9 would have been partly redone by the W1 daemon restructure.
