## Design forks for preflight (J-numbered; grounded recommendations the ACs assume) (story lines 69-105)

**J1 — WHERE THE CHAIN RIDES: the leaf (inside the convergence hash) or the bundle (outside it)? ⚠ THIS IS THE STORY'S CENTRAL FORK AND ITS BIGGEST RISK (E1).** `replication/leaf.rs:57-60` states, as a design decision with a stated rationale, that provenance is **intentionally excluded** from the canonical form because *"two regions may reference different local log entries for the same logical row and must still converge."* The leaf canonical form **is** the 11.2a convergence oracle.
- **(a) Bundle-only.** Provenance is signed but lives outside per-row canonical bytes. **Reject.** The bundle is transient; the store row persists. Provenance would be **lost at apply**, which is precisely the failure §15.3 `:66` demands be avoided (*"the provenance copy lands with the row, so ordinary traceback dereferences within the consumer team's own database"*). Round-2 of 13.3 killed the same shape for attestation.
- **(b) Leaf v3.** **RECOMMEND.** Provenance enters the canonical pre-image under `KV_LEAF_DOMAIN_V3`, is covered by the region/team signature, and survives apply. **Three conditions, all mandatory:** (i) **ADR-058 explicitly amends the `replication/leaf.rs:57-60` rationale** — the exclusion was written for a *region-local, re-derivable* reference; a **verbatim-copied, never-re-derived** cross-team chain is a different object. Amend the comment **and** the ADR; do not let the code comment silently contradict the shipped design. (ii) **The chain is copied verbatim at apply and NEVER re-derived at the destination** — it cannot be, `flatten_source_log_ref` has no cross-store selector (E2/`distillate.rs:196-199`), so any re-derivation attempt is also a correctness bug. (iii) **Convergence for provenance-carrying rows is proven live on the real two-region substrate**, not argued. If (iii) reds, the fork was decided wrong and it is J1(a) plus a separate persistence column — raise it, do not weaken the assertion.
- **(c) Leaf v3 + a nullable store column, provenance excluded from the hash.** Carried but **unsigned** — the shape this codebase has killed three times (13.2's trusted-applied-root registry, 13.3's Merkle-proof-without-signature). **Reject.**

**J2 — WHAT AUTHORIZES A CROSS-TEAM CITATION (E2 is the constraint).** The epic says "widen citer-auth to the team axis"; **there is no team axis in the TL to widen along** (`TransparencyLogEntry :194-206` has no team; `grep -rni team crates/maos-iac/src/` = **0**).
- **(a) Add a team column to the TL and resolve pid→team.** **Reject for this story** — that is `maos-iac` schema work, and the epic assigns it to **13.5e** by name (`epic-13:168,187`, *"not ZERO — `maos-iac` schema + `maos-audit` path resolution"*). Building it here duplicates 13.5e badly and inside the wrong kernel-delta budget.
- **(b) Authorize from the leaf-carried, verify-proved `source_team`.** **RECOMMEND.** The ratified rule: *a cross-principal citation is admitted **only** when the cited frame's provenance arrived through a **verified v3 leaf** whose `source_team` was proved by `verify_replication_bundle`, **and** an ordered-pair `(from_team → to_team, intent)` grant covers it (13.3's `[[cross_team_consent]]`), **and** the citing Spirit's own team is that `to_team`.* All three conjuncts are already-verified artifacts — **no new identity source, no pid lookup, no TL schema change.** Absence of any conjunct → refusal, not admission. **Fail closed by default**: the existing `e.spirit_pid != spirit_pid` arm stays the rule and the new arm is a **narrow, additively-guarded exception**, never a relaxation of the predicate.
- **⚠ The honesty obligation, non-negotiable:** `distillate.rs:330-338`'s comment states the control's purpose. **Update that comment in the same commit as the widening**, and record in ADR-058 exactly what class of laundering is now possible that was not before. A widened control with a stale comment claiming it is narrow is the single worst artifact this story could ship.
- **⚠ Do not widen the intermediate-hop gap by accident.** The loop runs over raw leaves only (`:242-245,:339`). Widening the leaf predicate must not be read as re-verifying hops it never verified.

**J3 — WHERE CROSS-WALL RECALL CONSENT IS DECIDED.** `maos-iac` is **team-blind** (grep = 0) and its `Cargo.toml` carries **only** `maos-domain`, `maos-spirit-abi`, `maos-capability`, `maos-attrs` — **no `maos-cohort`, no `maos-loom-lite`**.
- **RECOMMEND: a `CrossWallRecallConsentPort` trait in `maos-domain`, implemented in `maos-bin` over `CohortManifestState`, injected into `LogRecallAdapter` by a builder.** Rationale: **`TeamId` already lives in `maos-domain/src/team.rs:11`**, so `maos-iac` can *already* see it through its existing edge — **zero new dependency edges**. This is 13.1's `TenantMapPort` idiom (`loom-lite/src/tenant.rs:22-26` + `maos-bin/src/tenant_map.rs:105`) and 13.3's `CrossTeamConsentPort`, applied a third time. **Reject** a `maos-iac`→`maos-cohort` edge (mirrors the edge ADR-055 `:70` forbids for loom-lite) and **reject** deciding consent in the A2A router (`cohort_consent_chokepoint_12_2.rs:12-26` grep-pins two counts; and this governs a **read**, not frame delivery).
- ⚠ **`check-dependency-closure` will NOT catch a bad `maos-iac` edge** — `CHECKED_CRATES = ["maos-kernel-core", "maos-domain"]` (`check_dependency_closure.rs:65`) and it only looks for `FORBIDDEN_CRATES` (sqlx/postgres/wasmtime). Verify by hand with `cargo tree`.

**J4 — THE SHAPE OF THE `LogRecallPort` CHANGE (E11 is the constraint).** Kernel-core names the **concrete** `LogRecallAdapter` at six lines (`api.rs:40`; `kernel_ctx.rs:27,85`; `scheduler_loop.rs:96`; `hook_dispatch.rs:60,117`).
- **RECOMMEND: (i) an additive third trait method** — `recall_cross_wall(&self, spirit_pid, team: &TeamId, filter) -> Result<LogRecallPage, LogRecallError>` — leaving `recall`/`fetch` **byte-identical**, so every existing impl and the `spirits/researcher` contract are untouched; **(ii) a private field + `with_cross_wall_consent(...)` builder** on `LogRecallAdapter`, copying the shipped `with_isolation_hook` idiom verbatim (`log_recall.rs:74`), so `LogRecallAdapter::new`'s **one-argument** signature is preserved across all **38** call sites (37 test + the single production site `main.rs:2417`).
- **⚠ FORBIDDEN, each for a named reason:** a **generic parameter** on `LogRecallAdapter` (moves 6 kernel-core lines → baseline dead); a **rename** (same); a **required new argument to `new()`** (compiles nowhere until 38 sites move, 12 of them in `crates/maos-kernel-core/tests/` — those do **not** count toward the baseline, which reads `crates/maos-kernel-core/src` only, but they red the build); **changing the existing `recall`/`fetch` signatures** (breaks the 8.1→8.2 researcher contract for zero gain).
- **Verify before writing code:** `cargo check -p maos-kernel-core` against the planned shape. The ABI gates are a **null control** here — `abi_diff.rs:8` and `check_abi_ratification.rs:126` both hard-code `crates/maos-spirit-abi/Cargo.toml` only.

**J5 — THE REFUSAL VARIANT'S NAME, AND WHETHER IT IS CATALOGED.** `LogRecallError` (`maos-domain/src/log_recall.rs:248-268`) has five variants, **none E-prefixed**, and the enum is **not `#[non_exhaustive]`** — but nothing matches it wildcard-free outside its own crate, so a new variant is safe (E11).
- ⚠ **`crates/maos-domain/src` IS inside `error-catalog.toml` `scan_dirs` (`:29`)** — the **opposite** of 13.3's H8 posture, where `maos-loom-lite` sits outside. `is_e_prefixed` (`check_error_catalog.rs:97-99`) is `(Some('E'), Some(c)) if c.is_ascii_uppercase()`, applied **per variant**.
- **RECOMMEND: E-prefixed and cataloged** — e.g. `ECrossWallRecallDenied` — with a full `[[error]]` entry (all 6 metadata fields; `min_entry_count` is a floor, so adding is safe). Rationale: a first-class, caller-legible refusal is **this AC's entire point**, FR63 enforces it mechanically for free, and **the precedent for an E-prefixed variant inside a non-E-prefixed enum already ships**: `IacBusError::EIntentLineageBroken` (`error-catalog.toml`, `rust_path = "IacBusError::EIntentLineageBroken"`).

## Dev Notes (story lines 500-564)

### The one-paragraph version
This story writes the decision the project has been citing for months and never made. Everything hard about it is **honesty about what does not exist**: the leaf has **no provenance fields and an explicit design comment saying it never should** (E1); the TL has **no team axis** to widen the citer-auth check along (E2); the `log.recall` authority everyone cites **was never written** (E3); v2's canonical bytes have **no guard of any kind** (E5); the "compile-time pin" protecting `ranged_recall` is **a doc comment** (E7); and provenance is **already being silently destroyed by redaction on the local path** (E12). The traps are (1) adding provenance to the convergence hash without deciding J1 and proving convergence live; (2) relaxing the citer-auth predicate instead of adding a conjunctive, fail-closed exception — and leaving its comment stale; (3) generic-izing or renaming `LogRecallAdapter`, which moves **six** kernel-core lines; (4) touching `to_canonical_bytes` before the v2 golden exists; (5) leaving `Some(team) => V2` unguarded so a v3 leaf emits a v2 tag with v3 bytes; (6) reaching for `ranged_recall`; (7) reusing `InvalidCursor` for a consent refusal; and (8) writing any sentence, anywhere, that says this establishes authorization provenance. **If you find yourself editing `crates/maos-kernel-core/src`, adding a generic to `LogRecallAdapter`, creating a `maos-iac`→`maos-cohort` edge, or making the citer-auth check *less* strict rather than *conditionally* wider — stop; the design has gone wrong.**

### Traps (each is proven live at HEAD or has already bitten this codebase)
1. **Adding provenance to the convergence hash without deciding J1.** `replication/leaf.rs:57-60` excludes it **by design, with a stated rationale**. Decide it, amend it, prove convergence live (E1).
2. **Touching `to_canonical_bytes` before the v2 golden exists.** There is **no v2 oracle** and the one test that claims to guard the v2 length prefix **cannot** (E5). You will not know.
3. **Leaving `Some(team) => KV_LEAF_DOMAIN_V2` unguarded.** A team-carrying v3 leaf then emits the **v2 tag with v3 tail bytes**. Silent. Not a compile error (E6). This is `manifest.rs:235`'s shape.
4. **"Widening citer-auth to the team axis."** There is no team axis in the TL (E2). Authorize from the **verified leaf**, not a pid lookup.
5. **Relaxing the citer-auth predicate instead of adding a conjunctive exception.** And leaving `distillate.rs:330-338`'s comment claiming the control is still narrow.
6. **Generic-izing or renaming `LogRecallAdapter`.** Six kernel-core lines move; baseline dead (E11). A method, a private field, or an error variant is free.
7. **Adding a required argument to `LogRecallAdapter::new`.** 38 call sites; use the `with_isolation_hook` builder idiom.
8. **Naming the refusal variant `E*` without a catalog entry** — `maos-domain/src` **IS** in `scan_dirs` (`error-catalog.toml:29`), the **opposite** of 13.3's loom-lite posture. Or reusing `InvalidCursor`, which makes refusal look like a pagination fault.
9. **`ranged_recall` for anything.** Path-addressed, capability-free, and it **discards `spirit_filter` for TL rows on a false rationale** (E8).
10. **Calling the researcher pin "compile-time enforced."** It is a doc comment with no gate (E7). Real, ungated — say both.
11. **Assuming diamonds flatten.** They red as cycles (E9), and cross-team multi-hop produces them by construction.
12. **Repeating `distillate.rs:16-19`'s "halt at depth 3."** No such constant exists (E10).
13. **Making distillate emit compact-32 hex to "fix" the codec schism.** `redaction.rs:180-192` scrubs any ≥32 hex run; the colon format exists **because** of it (`distillate.rs:446-447`). Fix the digest decoder instead.

### Testing standards
Live legs `#[ignore]`-gated + `MAOS_TEST_POSTGRES_TEAM_A/_B`-driven + panic-if-unset; hermetic legs plain `cargo test`; each gate leg is exactly one `--exact` test carrying the vacuity guard (`"running 1 test"` + `"1 passed"`, `check_multi_tenant_loom.rs:91`). **The citer-auth three-way negative and the refusal-vs-empty-page distinction must be proven red through real code paths** — a stub cannot prove a control was widened correctly, only that a mock returns what it was told. Verifiers **independently derived from the write codec** with a **positive control** (never a re-call of the function under test, never a reject-everything stub). Goldens are **pinned hex with a comment naming what breaks**. §A7 reflexes per leg: derive-and-reconcile on admitted/refused counts; **prove the diamond fix red before fixing it**; freeze the v2 golden **before** v3 exists; a **skipped live leg is unmeasured, not green**.
