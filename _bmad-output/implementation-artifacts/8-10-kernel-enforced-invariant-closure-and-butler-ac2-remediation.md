There is **no** method on `Ctx` to call `set_scalar` or to drive the policy path. This is the SAME gap Mira (`spirits/mira/src/lib.rs:168-180`) and Observer (`spirits/observer/src/lib.rs:353-374`) navigate by storing-only. Story 8.1's `on_idle` did exactly the same store-only thing — and its review wrongly marked the halt "applied."

The halt-firing logic lives in `maos-kernel-core::capability::working_memory::orchestrator::WorkingMemoryOrchestrator::process_scalar_write(...)` (returns `Option<HaltReceipt>`). `corpus_halt.rs:188-202` already drives a real halt — but it calls `process_scalar_write` **directly, bypassing `on_idle`**. AC1's defect is precisely that production `on_idle` never reaches that path.
