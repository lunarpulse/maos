## What this story is, in one paragraph

13.1 built the **physical** wall, 13.2 the **cryptographic** wall at entry, 13.3 the **governed cross-team row**. All three move *bytes* across the wall. **None of them moves meaning.** A row that lands in team B carrying `source_team = A` is, to every reader in B, an assertion without a chain: the digest says "distilled from 14 prior decisions" and **there is no way to name those 14 decisions, let alone read them**. That is the Reza 4:55 PM scene, and at HEAD it does not reproduce.

**13.3b makes provenance cross the wall.** Three mechanisms, each of which does not exist at HEAD in any form:

- **The chain rides with the row.** `CollectiveKvLeaf` (`replication/leaf.rs:61-78`) has **no `source_log_ref`, no `distillation_depth`, no `intent_lineage`** — there is no wire representation of provenance anywhere in the replication path. A **leaf v3** adds one, under a third domain tag, with **v1 AND v2 canonical bytes byte-identical** — two frozen predecessors now, and (E5) **only the v1 half is currently guarded by anything at all**.
- **Cross-team citation becomes expressible under a ratified rule.** `distillate.rs:339-359` (Story 8.10 AC2b) rejects `CiterUnauthorized` on `e.spirit_pid != spirit_pid` over the **flattened** refs, explicitly *"so a digest-of-digest cannot launder a cross-principal raw frame through an intermediate hop."* This story's ask **is what that control was built to prevent**. It is widened by an **ADR-grade rule with its own negative control**, or not at all.
- **Raw traceback across the wall becomes a consented read.** `LogRecallPort` is 2 methods, `spirit_pid`-only; `recall` hard-pins emitter scope (`log_recall.rs:282`); the consent check is a **comment** (`:378-386`); `LogRecallError` has **no refusal variant**; and `grep -rni team crates/maos-iac/src/` returns **0** — the entire IAC spine is team-blind.

It also **writes the authority that everything above cites and that has never existed**: ADR-058, plus porting ADR-013 and ADR-018 into `docs/adr/`.

It does **not** build per-team TL isolation or the tenant-blind-audit closure (13.5e), collective erasure (13.5b), tenant-mode boot (13.5c), or the Spirit→collective route (13.5d).

## Story

As **Reza, running a 400-person fintech as one governed Cortex**, at 4:55 PM with a consolidated proposal in front of me that cites 14 prior schema decisions made by three different teams,

I want **the provenance chain to travel with the shared row — flattened to raw frames, depth-counted, intent-lineage-bound, and signed under a versioned domain tag — so that ordinary traceback resolves inside my own team's database; and when I need the raw frame itself from behind another team's wall, that read is an explicitly consented `log.recall` whose refusal reaches me as a first-class, named outcome rather than a missing row**,

so that **a cross-team digest is an auditable claim instead of an assertion — and the three ways this silently degrades into theatre (a provenance chain that is carried but unsigned, a citer-auth control relaxed as a side effect of making a test pass, and a refused cross-wall read that is indistinguishable from an empty result) are each closed by construction and proven red.**

## Design forks for preflight (J-numbered; grounded recommendations the ACs assume)

**J1 — WHERE THE CHAIN RIDES: the leaf (inside the convergence hash) or the bundle (outside it)? ⚠ THIS IS THE STORY'S CENTRAL FORK AND ITS BIGGEST RISK (E1).** `leaf.rs:57-60` states, as a design decision with a stated rationale, that provenance is **intentionally excluded** from the canonical form because *"two regions may reference different local log entries for the same logical row and must still converge."* The leaf canonical form **is** the 11.2a convergence oracle.
- **(a) Bundle-only.** Provenance is signed but lives outside per-row canonical bytes. **Reject.** The bundle is transient; the store row persists. Provenance would be **lost at apply**, which is precisely the failure §15.3 `:66` demands be avoided (*"the provenance copy lands with the row, so ordinary traceback dereferences within the consumer team's own database"*). Round-2 of 13.3 killed the same shape for attestation.
- **(b) Leaf v3.** **RECOMMEND.** Provenance enters the canonical pre-image under `KV_LEAF_DOMAIN_V3`, is covered by the region/team signature, and survives apply. **Three conditions, all mandatory:** (i) **ADR-058 explicitly amends the `leaf.rs:57-60` rationale** — the exclusion was written for a *region-local, re-derivable* reference; a **verbatim-copied, never-re-derived** cross-team chain is a different object. Amend the comment **and** the ADR; do not let the code comment silently contradict the shipped design. (ii) **The chain is copied verbatim at apply and NEVER re-derived at the destination** — it cannot be, `flatten_source_log_ref` has no cross-store selector (E2/`distillate.rs:196-199`), so any re-derivation attempt is also a correctness bug. (iii) **Convergence for provenance-carrying rows is proven live on the real two-region substrate**, not argued. If (iii) reds, the fork was decided wrong and it is J1(a) plus a separate persistence column — raise it, do not weaken the assertion.
- **(c) Leaf v3 + a nullable store column, provenance excluded from the hash.** Carried but **unsigned** — the shape this codebase has killed three times (13.2's trusted-applied-root registry, 13.3's Merkle-proof-without-signature). **Reject.**

**J2 — WHAT AUTHORIZES A CROSS-TEAM CITATION (E2 is the constraint).** The epic says "widen citer-auth to the team axis"; **there is no team axis in the TL to widen along** (`TransparencyLogEntry :194-206` has no team; `grep -rni team crates/maos-iac/src/` = **0**).
- **(a) Add a team column to the TL and resolve pid→team.** **Reject for this story** — that is `maos-iac` schema work, and the epic assigns it to **13.5e** by name (`epic-13:168,187`, *"not ZERO — `maos-iac` schema + `maos-audit` path resolution"*). Building it here duplicates 13.5e badly and inside the wrong kernel-delta budget.
- **(b) Authorize from the leaf-carried, verify-proved `source_team`.** **RECOMMEND.** The ratified rule: *a cross-principal citation is admitted **only** when the cited frame's provenance arrived through a **verified v3 leaf** whose `source_team` was proved by `verify_replication_bundle`, **and** an ordered-pair `(from_team → to_team, intent)` grant covers it (13.3's `[[cross_team_consent]]`), **and** the citing Spirit's own team is that `to_team`.* All three conjuncts are already-verified artifacts — **no new identity source, no pid lookup, no TL schema change.** Absence of any conjunct → refusal, not admission. **Fail closed by default**: the existing `e.spirit_pid != spirit_pid` arm stays the rule and the new arm is a **narrow, additively-guarded exception**, never a relaxation of the predicate.
- **⚠ The honesty obligation, non-negotiable:** `distillate.rs:330-338`'s comment states the control's purpose. **Update that comment in the same commit as the widening**, and record in ADR-058 exactly what class of laundering is now possible that was not before. A widened control with a stale comment claiming it is narrow is the single worst artifact this story could ship.
- **⚠ Do not widen the intermediate-hop gap by accident.** The loop runs over raw leaves only (`:242-245,:339`). Widening the leaf predicate must not be read as re-verifying hops it never verified.

**J3 — WHERE CROSS-WALL RECALL CONSENT IS DECIDED.** `maos-iac` is **team-blind** (grep = 0) and its `Cargo.toml` carries **only** `maos-domain`, `maos-spirit-abi`, `maos-capability`, `maos-attrs` — **no `maos-cohort`, no `maos-loom-lite`**.
- **RECOMMEND: a `CrossWallRecallConsentPort` trait in `maos-domain`, implemented in `maos-bin` over `CohortManifestState`, injected into `LogRecallAdapter` by a builder.** Rationale: **`TeamId` already lives in `maos-domain/src/team.rs:11`**, so `maos-iac` can *already* see it through its existing edge — **zero new dependency edges**. This is 13.1's `TenantMapPort` idiom (`loom-lite/src/tenant.rs:22-26` + `maos-bin/src/tenant_map.rs:105`) and 13.3's `CrossTeamConsentPort`, applied a third time. **Reject** a `maos-iac`→`maos-cohort` edge (mirrors the edge ADR-055 `:70` forbids for loom-lite) and **reject** deciding consent in the A2A router (`cohort_consent_chokepoint_12_2.rs:12-26` grep-pins two counts; and this governs a **read**, not frame delivery).
