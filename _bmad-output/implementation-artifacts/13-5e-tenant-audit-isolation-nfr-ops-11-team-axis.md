## What this story is, in one paragraph

13.1 built the **physical** wall (database-per-team). 13.2 built the **cryptographic** wall at entry. 13.3 made the **row** real. 13.5c makes the wall **bootable** and collapses the daemon to **one** Transparency Log. All four of them are about the **store**. **None of them touched the audit spine, and the audit spine has never heard of any of it** — `grep -rn "team\|tenant\|operator_id" crates/maos-iac/src/` returns **zero** against a directory of 15 files and 7,421 lines where the control grep `spirit` returns **391** (N1). The Transparency Log's only isolation dimension is `spirit_pid`, and its two read surfaces are worse than un-scoped: `maos_audit::query` is **path-addressed and capability-free** — whoever can name the file reads every row of every team — and `ranged_recall` **silently discards the caller's `spirit_filter`** on a **stated rationale that is factually false** (N2).

**13.5e closes the audit axis on the team axis, and closes it the way ADR-055 already decided storage boundaries get closed:** *"Shared database/table plus team predicate: rejected; one omitted predicate defeats the wall"* (`ADR-055:80`). The shard is therefore a **path**, not a column — and that turns out to be the only shape that survives the kernel budget, because `insert_frame_event(` has **35 call sites inside `crates/maos-kernel-core/src` across 20 files** (N5). A team parameter on the TL write API is a 35-line kernel-core delta; a team-derived **path** chosen at the composition root is **zero**.

It does **not** build per-**operator** anything. **NFR-Ops-11 is served on the TEAM axis and is NOT closed** — sub-axes (i) namespace and (iii) capability-token signing key are operator-axis and deferred past v2.2; (iv) erasure scope is **13.5b's**. It does not build the Spirit→collective route (13.5d), collective erasure (13.5b), or cross-wall `log.recall` (13.3b).

## Story

As a **MAOS operator running Reza's 400-person fintech as one governed Cortex (teams `security`, `support`, `data`)**,

I want **each team's Transparency Log to be a physically separate artifact selected by the team the process serves — never a shared table filtered by a predicate — and both read surfaces into it to be scoped by construction rather than by the caller's good intentions: `maos_audit::query` addressable only through a team-resolved path, and `ranged_recall`'s discarded `spirit_filter` honoured against the `spirit_pid` column the log has carried since v0.1**,

so that **a cross-team raw-log read cannot succeed by knowing a filesystem path, a `--spirit` flag means what its help text says, and the tenant wall 13.1/13.2/13.3 built around the store is not undone by the audit spine sitting beside it — with the three ways this silently degrades into theatre (a per-team log that no production writer ever writes to, a team check the two raw-SQL TL bypasses walk straight past, and an "isolation" claim resting on operator env-var discipline rather than on code) each closed by construction and proven red.**

## Design forks for preflight (M-numbered; grounded recommendations the ACs assume)

**M1 — THE FORK: file-per-team vs. column-plus-predicate.**
- **(a) File-per-team** — the TL path is derived from the team the process serves; `TransparencyLogAdapter` stays byte-identical; kernel-core stays team-blind and is simply pointed at a different file.
- **(b) Column-plus-predicate** — add a `team` column to `transparency_log` and a `team` field to `FrameFilter`; every read predicates on it.
- **✅ Recommend (a), on three independent grounds — and (b) is not merely worse, it is unaffordable on one side and unenforceable on the other.**
  1. **Precedent.** `ADR-055:80` verbatim: *"Shared database/table plus team predicate: rejected; one omitted predicate defeats the wall."* Context `:14`: *"one omitted `WHERE` clause would cross the boundary."* The TL has **more** read paths than the store, not fewer — 31 path-resolution sites (N9) and two raw-SQL bypasses (N11) — so the omitted-predicate risk is strictly higher here.
  2. **Kernel budget (N5).** (b)'s *read* half is free — `FrameFilter` derives `Default` and every kernel-core literal spreads it. Its **write** half is not: populating a `team` column means a parameter on `insert_frame_event`, which is **35 call sites across 20 kernel-core files**, plus `open_in_memory` at 11. That is a FLAG-Winston re-pin and it kills the ZERO claim. (a) binds team at `open()` — and kernel-core **never calls `open()` in production** (N7).
  3. **Shape (N7).** One process = one `home_team` = one store = one pool. The team is a *process* property. A per-row column would encode, on every row, a fact that is constant for the whole process lifetime.
- **⚠ Name (a)'s real costs, do not hide them:** seven tables split, not one (M3); N Merkle roots where NFR-Ops-9's verifier is 1:1 (M8); cross-team queries lose a single source (accept — that is the *point*); and SQLite `busy_timeout=5000` multi-writer contention (`transparency_log.rs:20-35`) becomes per-file.
- **⚠ (b) is not available as a fallback "just for reads."** A read predicate over an unpopulated column is a filter over a NULL — worse than no control, because it looks like one.

**M2 — where the team enters path resolution, and the H-ORDER conflict (N8, N9).**
`default_transparency_log_path()` takes zero arguments (`maos-audit/src/lib.rs:853`) — there is nowhere to put a team. Options:
- **(a) A sibling `transparency_log_path_for_team(&TeamId) -> PathBuf`** in `maos-audit`, with the existing zero-arg function retained for the untenanted default. Callers that must be team-scoped move to the new one.
- **(b) Read `MAOS_LOOM_HOME_TEAM` *inside* the existing function.** Zero call-site churn; also zero visibility — the addressing becomes invisible ambient state.
- **(c) Leave the path global and rely on the operator setting `MAOS_AUDIT_DB` per team process.** **Reject.** This is *achievable today with no code at all*, which is exactly why it is not isolation: it is operator convention, unenforced, and a team-blind read remains one `env` away. It would also make the AC4 negative unfalsifiable.
- **✅ Recommend (a)**, with the three-chokepoint plan the call graph actually supports (N9): `maos-shell` **2 sites** (`lib.rs:90,108`), `maos-bin` **5 sites**, and `maos-cli` **one wrapper** (`subcommands.rs:3048-3049`) covering all 24 of its sites. **Say "three chokepoints", not "31 call sites".**
- **⚠ H-ORDER (N8) MUST BE RESOLVED HERE, NOT DISCOVERED.** `audit_db_path` is resolved at `main.rs:2057`; `home_team` is read at `:2282`; 13.5c's window is `:2254`–`:2274`. Sub-fork:
