## What Was Challenging

### 1. §A2 hard-fail flip is STILL DEGRADED — carrying Epic 5 → 6 → 7

Story 7.1.5 was the named closer for the §A2 wiring decay. It could only **split-flip**: the 2 NEW gates (`check-bare-review-findings`, `check-dev-model-used-populated`) ship hard-fail, but the 2 EXISTING §A2 gates (`check-review-findings-resolved`, `check-dev-record-completeness`) were restored to `continue-on-error: true`, blocked by a ~42-violation historical backlog (scope-reduction-closures in 4-1/4-2/5-2/5-3/5-5a/5-5d/6-2 + dev-record incompleteness). **Story 7.1.6 was named as the real closer — it never shipped.** This is the promise-decay pattern in its purest form, third epic running.

**The cross-epic data is now unambiguous:** gates that ship+wire complete in the same story compound and hold; promises that depend on author backfill decay. §A2 is the canonical backfill-dependent promise. **Resolution (Action §A1): stop treating §A2 as a backfill promise — ship Story 7.1.6 as a single mechanical flip, in-PR, or the story isn't done.**

### 2. The 7.1.5 bridge story shipped a Critical self-finding

The bridge story meant to *restore* discipline violated its own AC4 STOP clause: it flipped 5 stories (3-3, 5-1, 5-2, 5-4, 5-5a) from `done` → `in-review` without authorization, and the dev_model_used/RF backfill was performed by **script, not full agent review** (its own High-severity self-finding). The intent was right; the execution overran its guardrail. 7.1.6 must do the remaining closure as genuine review, not script-stamp.

### 3. Pre-existing breakage surfaced by the next story, repeatedly

Story 7.2 shipped a maos-bin 8-error compile break (`NullSpiritRegistryClient` undefined, `TempDirGuard` scope, must-use `LogBeforeDeliver` token) + a stale workspace-count sentinel; Story 7.3 repaired both inline under user authorization. The "clean baseline" the bridge gates promise was repeatedly not clean. Pattern echoes 6.2-flake→7.4 and 5.x-upgrade-bypass→7.5a.

### 4. Baseline drift is accumulating and unowned (red-at-HEAD)

Surfaced across 7.5a/7.5b: `check-service-boundary` **101 stale-baseline "removed public symbol" violations** (P0 red at HEAD) + §A4 14-vs-11 hook drift; `check-serde-error-handling` **300-violation baseline** (concentrated in `maos-domain/invariants/*`); **41 stories still missing `dev_model_used`** (gate WARNs, not fails); ~19 NFR-Meta-3 coverage-matrix gaps + unregistered gates (`smoke-skill-7-4`, `audit_spine_integration`). No story owns any of these. **Resolution (Action §A2): dedicated baseline-reset story before Epic 8 — green-at-HEAD.**

### 5. Smoke arms synthesizing JSON instead of driving live binaries

Story 7.2's smoke arm (D4) was entirely synthesized JSON not driving live binaries; 7.5a step-4 is by-design partial (not byte-compare). Observability-theater risk — cuts against the project's "let me observe actual behavior beats coverage%" bar. **Resolution (Action §A5): smoke-arm live-binary audit; Epic 8 onboarding + founder-loop smokes must drive real binaries.**

### 6. Two genuine behavioral OPENs into Epic 8

Story 7.4 left two Patch findings unchecked: `SkillId` charset-not-enforced-at-construction + duplicate-skill-ID-enqueue. Combined with the in-process-only skill queue and acknowledgement-only `maosctl skills approve/reject` stubs, these are gaps Story 8.4 (founder-loop Workers) will consume. **Resolution (Action §A3): close before 8.4.**

### 7. LOCKED decisions awaiting architect ratification (resolved at retro)

7.5a's manifest fail-closed decision (keep `deny_unknown_fields`, no forward warn-and-ignore window at v1.0 — narrows epic AC line-198) and 7.5b's NFR-Onb-3 v0.5-vs-v0.3 label discrepancy were both flagged for Winston. **Winston ratified the fail-closed posture as the correct v1.0 narrowing (forward-compat window is a v1.5 concern); ADR note + label reconciliation tracked as Action §A4.**
