### 6. Story 5.5d "Critical findings closed by SCOPE REDUCTION" antipattern

Several of Story 5.5d's "closed" findings have closure resolutions that read like scope reductions rather than fixes. Examples:
- 5.5c Finding #3 (StdioTransport persistent-child violation): "Persistent subprocess with JoinHandle self-prune deferred to v0.5-α follow-up; per-call spawn is acceptable for v0.5-α initial substrate" — i.e., the AC1 requirement was renegotiated post-implementation.
- 5.5d Finding #5-decision (AC6 deliverables absent): closure marked "Implement Tasks 15 & 16: ..." — i.e., the closure plan is "ship MORE work in this story before merging" but sprint-status is already `done`. The closure is forward-looking, not retrospective.
- 5.5d Finding #4-decision (production path dead): closure marked "Wire 4-way switch: stub → FixtureReplay / empty → Null / file:// → LocalFs / otherwise → McpSpiritRegistryClient" — same forward-shape issue.

**The pattern**: when a critical finding's fix is non-trivial, the closure resolution PLANS THE FIX rather than EXECUTING IT, and the status is marked `closed` based on the plan rather than the diff. This is a corruption of the Epic 2 retro A6 status-column discipline — `closed` is supposed to mean "resolved in this PR", not "planned for a follow-up".

**Action item §A5 closes this:** add a `xtask check-review-findings-resolved` gate asserting (a) every finding marked `closed` references at least one commit-touched file in the story's File List; (b) status `open` blocks sprint-status from advancing to `done`. The status column must be load-bearing.

### 7. The 5.5b commit is missing from `git log HEAD -20`

`git log --oneline -20` walking back from HEAD shows commits for 5-5a, 5-4, 5-3, 5-2, 5-1, 4-5, 4-4, 4-3, 4-2, 4-1, and earlier — but NO commit named after 5-5b (multi-provider). Story 5.5b file artifacts exist on disk (`crates/maos-providers/openai.rs`, `crates/maos-providers/ollama.rs`, fixtures), the smoke-multi-provider-5 arm exists in `main.rs`, and sprint-status marks 5-5b `done`. The commit either was rolled into 5-5a (duplicate 2916b84 vs 248f23b on the same story name) OR exists earlier in history outside the 20-commit window.

**Why it matters:** Story-to-commit traceability is part of the audit trail. If 5-5b lacks a dedicated commit, the diff that constitutes 5-5b's substantive change is conflated with another story's commit. This makes per-story review difficult and post-hoc bisection harder.

**Action item §A7 closes this:** investigate 5-5b's commit attribution; if rolled into another story's commit, document the conflation. Future-forward: story-commit 1:1 mapping should be enforced via the bmad-dev-story workflow.

## Closing Note

Epic 5 delivers nine stories of Spirit lifecycle, hot-swap, crash supervision, multi-provider, MCP/ACP, T3 sandbox, registry, and §13.1 measurement substrate in roughly four wall-clock days. The substrate-extension pattern compounded — Story 5.1's `SpiritControlBlock` consumed by 5.2/5.3/5.4/5.5a; Story 5.4's `RegistryClient` trait consumed by 5.5d; Story 5.5c's `McpClient::call` consumed by 5.5d; the smoke-arm pattern proliferated into 9 runnable Layer-1.5 observability bridges. The substrate works on every story where it was reviewed.

**But Epic 5 is the first epic in MAOS history where review discipline collapsed.** Six of nine stories shipped without formal code-review — Story 5.5d shipped `done` with 8 Critical OPEN findings (production registry path dead, yank propagation non-functional, compliance fingerprint check tautological). Epic 4 retro's §A6 anti-pattern (serde unwrap_or_default) recurred 8 separate times in 5.5d alone because the mechanical gate Epic 4 promised was never built. Three pre-existing discipline-gate failures (`check-empty-kernel` 64 violations, `check-service-boundary` baseline drift, kernel-core KLOC overshoot) sat "inherited not regression" for five consecutive epics and are now accumulating ADR-038 violation pressure.

The pattern is clear: **action items that ship a mechanical gate compound; action items that promise to ship a gate decay.** Epic 4 retro §A1 (smoke-epic-4 arm) shipped and the smoke-arm pattern proliferated to 9 arms in Epic 5. Epic 4 retro §A4/§A5 (pub-field-constructors + composition-root-completeness gates) shipped and caught regressions. Epic 4 retro §A6 (serde error-handling lint) and §A7 (dev-record truthfulness gate) were PROMISES and produced 8 + 4 recurrences respectively.

Lunarpulse's evaluation discipline `[[feedback_lunarpulse_observability_preference]]` ("when can I observe actual behavior beats coverage%") was honored at the smoke-arm layer (9 arms = 9 observable surfaces) but VIOLATED at the review-discipline layer — 6 of 9 stories shipped without the structured-review observability that catches integration-boundary bugs. The smoke-arm proves the happy path works; the review pass catches the failure paths the smoke arm doesn't exercise. **Both are required.**

Action items §A1 (5.5d critical-open remediation) and §A2 (5-story review backfill) are the bridge to Story 6.1. §A3/§A5/§A6 build the three mechanical gates that Epic 4 retro promised plus the new gates this epic discovered. §A4 elevates three inherited debts to a decision point. §A7 captures the glm-5.1 model profile. §A8 resolves the 5-5b commit-attribution gap.

Story 5.5e's §13.1 measurement gate spec is the densest decision-substrate artifact in Epic 5 — 713 lines describing how to run the bench, write ADR-040, and either defer rust-inproc to v2.0+ or unlock it in v0.5. **The decision is data-driven, not architectural. The substrate's "falsifiable architecture" claim becomes mechanically true when the dev runs `cargo bench -p maos-bench` and ADR-040 lands with status `accepted`.** Story 5.5e is implementation-pending; its outcome flows forward to Story 10.2's NFR-Test-7 scope and Story 6.2's CliWrapperSpirit form choice.

Epic 5 closes on a mixed note. The structural substrate (lifecycle + hot-swap + crash + multi-provider + MCP/ACP + T3 + registry baseline + §13.1 spec) is in place. The quality substrate has gaps. The bridge work (§A1 + §A2) is non-trivial but bounded. Story 6.1 opens once both close.
