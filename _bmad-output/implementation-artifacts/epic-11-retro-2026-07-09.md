## 2. What Went Well

1. **The §A6 review net is the story of the epic — it caught a genuine ship-blocker on all 11 stories.** Not soft ones: a fake echo-loop runner + absent T2 cell + unwired composition root (11.1a) under 87 green tests; an xtask stub printing `passed: true` without invoking the oracle (11.1b); a `+=0` tautological falsifier (11.2b); three real mechanisms dead-wired at the daemon (11.4c); and — the sharpest irony — **the two stories whose entire thesis is "kill canned green" (11.5, 11.7) shipped canned-green machinery themselves** (synthetic snapshots, `Path::exists` for byte-identity, an honor-system provenance string you could `vim` to green). The net is the real control.
2. **Party-mode preflight consistently out-performed the written spec.** 11.4b **CATCH-0** inverted the story's own kernel-Δ premise *down to zero* before a line was written; 11.2a surfaced the audit-orphan ship-blocker and the honest "consensus"→"convergent replication" rename; 11.4a overruled a ZERO-Δ lean on three correctness grounds (forbid-as-approval bypass); Grumbal killed a hidden kernel edit in 11.4c.
3. **Kernel-Δ discipline held.** 11.1a spent zero of its 150-LOC headroom. Every re-pin named its authorized surface in `kernel-core-baseline.toml` HISTORY. Tripwire caught zero accidental overflow.
4. **Honesty discipline is now cultural.** NFR-Sec-3 kept at "1 of 3 signals @v2.0" (not claimed satisfied); 11.5/11.7 shipped infra-only with genuine-external cohorts deferred to v2.5 and the honesty clause written *into the requirement text*; environment limits (seccomp-blocked host) surfaced as marker-gated advisory-RED, never faked green.
5. **The identity-reflex family propagated cleanly:** form-identity → region-identity → identity-source → admission-source → attestation-source — each derived from the live emitting process, each with its own proven-red, each redding when the source is blinded.

## 3. What Was Hard / Systemic

1. **Model-tier policy drifted again — harder than Epic 10 (three epics deep).** Only 11.0 and 11.1a ran on opus-4-8; 11.1b/11.2a on opus-4-6, 11.2b/11.4a/11.4b on glm-5.2, 11.4c/11.5/11.7 on gpt-5.5. 11.2b is the story we *re-ratified opus-4-8 at preflight, overruling sprint-status* — then dev'd on glm-5.2. It was disclosed honestly every time, and the §A6 net was the compensating control. **Verdict: the net was the real control all along.** → resolved this retro (A1).
2. **First dev pass materially incomplete is the norm, not the exception.** 11.1a/11.1b ~22 patches each; 11.4a needed a distinct "objective reopen" + a whole new Task 8 to wire fail-closed into the daemon; 11.7 took two full review rounds. Green local suites repeatedly masked absent capability. Budget the second adversarial pass — the story files even pre-warn it.
3. **CI-execution gap recurs and keeps getting deferred.** 11.1a shipped tests "no CI job ran" (caught + fixed); 11.2b's live Postgres legs "never run in CI" was *deferred* (W2); 11.7's v2.0 consumer enforcement "never fires in CI." "Passes locally, ungated in CI" is a compounding debt. → resolved this retro (A2).
4. **One kernel re-pin overshot its ratified ceiling.** 11.2a was authorized +≤16 LOC but landed +59 (23023) because in-`src` `#[cfg(test)]` modules count toward the baseline. Discipline held (disclosed), but the preflight LOC estimate was mis-calibrated. → A6.
5. **"Done at story level" ≠ "shippable" — again.** Epic 11 is code-complete on `epic-11` but unmerged, still gated by the two external v1.5 holds (pen-test, export counsel). No Epic 12 defined yet.

## 9. Key Takeaways

1. **The §A6 adversarial review net is the project's real quality control** — it backstopped a model-tier policy we never followed and caught a genuine ship-blocker on every story, including canned-green machinery inside the anti-canned-green stories. Gate the control that works (A1), not the one on paper.

The countermeasures (derive-and-reconcile, real-subsystem proven-red, feature-flag ≠ measurement, "planted lie must turn it red") are now reflexes — apply them to every v2.2 gate.
3. **Founder call = ratified plan.** Functionality-first is the v2.2 phase; external holds are already non-gating for it. Merge feature-complete now; morph legal/security in later via the GA ledger.
4. **The roadmap's real start-gate is architecture, not code.** Ratify §15.10 before minting Epic 12 so J3/Reza don't discover the N-host-topology and multi-tenant-Loom foundations mid-story.
