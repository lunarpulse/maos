## Story

As a **MAOS release manager / v2.0 ship-gate owner**,
I want the **v2.0 black-box third-party-trial infrastructure** — a CI-bot harness that, on a **hermetic clean environment**, **re-loads** a candidate signed Spirit binary, **derives** its SBOM completeness and **verifies** its signing chain, and **measures** its halt-recall against the reference corpus,
so that **the N=12 trial's per-participant success facts (`binary_loads`, `frames_run`, `halt_recall`, `sbom_verified`, `signing_chain_verified`) are machine-DERIVED (never self-reported)** and the v2.0 ship gate can trust them — validated at v2.0 by the **in-house Chinese-wall proxy cohort** as proof-of-mechanism, with the **genuine external N=12 cohort deferred to v2.5** (Epic 14).

**The gap this closes (the "10.2 canned-trap"):** Story 10.2 shipped the *human-trial* protocol + results schema + the `check-third-party-trial` gate, but that gate is a **schema-validator over a hand-authored `trial-results.toml`** — every field (`binary_loads`, `frames_run`, `halt_recall`, `sbom_verified`, `signing_chain_verified`) is **typed in by a coordinator**, not produced by any machinery. At N=12/v1.0 that was deliberate (10.2 §5 / F4→A: *"PR review is the trust boundary at N=12"*; SBOM+signing were recorded but explicitly **not asserted**). At **v2.0**, NFR-Test-8 requires the record be **"auditable via SBOM + signing chain re-loaded on clean VM by CI bot"** — i.e. the facts must be **re-derived by a bot on a clean environment**. This story builds that derivation layer and its gate. It is the direct analogue of 11.5's de-canning of the FKCS self-report into a diff-oracle.

### Explicitly NOT in 11.7 (defend at review)

- **Recruiting or running the genuine external N=12 human trial** — v2.5, Epic 14 (`full-prd-gap-map…md:75`, epic-11…md:66). 11.7 ships **infrastructure only**; the populated external cohort is v2.5.
- **The 3 genuine external-authored Spirits** — v2.5 (shared deferral with 11.5). The ≥1 Spirits exercised here are **in-house Chinese-wall proxy authors**, never claimed as genuine externals.
- **Making the Chinese-wall proxy score a BLOCKING floor** — the v2.0 proxy score is **advisory proof-of-mechanism only** (RATIFIED F5/D8, Murat's two-things guard; mirrors 11.5 D6 / ADR-052 §4). The gates BLOCK on the *derivation*-is-real legs, never on the proxy cohort floor.
- **Emitting a CycloneDX/SPDX SBOM document** — DEFERRED to v2.5 (F4/D5a); at v2.0 the `cargo tree`/`Cargo.lock` closure IS the bill of materials. v2.5 is when genuine externals ship an SBOM you didn't compute.
- **The consent/NDA legal instrument** — counsel-owned, v2.5 (10.2 README §6 is an explicit placeholder stub; do not finalize it here).
- **A literal per-participant cloud-VM provisioner** — the v2.0 "clean VM by CI bot" is a **hermetic CI runner** (RATIFIED F3/D4). A literal spun-up VM per human participant is the v2.5 human protocol.
- **Any `maos-kernel-core` change** — ZERO kernel-Δ (verify at prep).
- **cert-body engagement / ≥20 registry Spirits / FR37 vetter issuance** — v2.5, DevRel/BD-driven (epic-11…md:66).

## ⚠️ PREFLIGHT FLAGS (landmines — read first)

- **L1 — The gate already exists; do NOT re-implement it.** `check-third-party-trial` (`xtask/src/check_third_party_trial.rs`, Story 10.2) already parses the schema, rejects negative counts / malformed dates / impossible counts, enumerates the cohort (`participant.len() != participants_total` → fail), reconciles `derived_successes` vs reported, enforces N=12 + ≥10 floors + stratification floors, and logs an advisory Wilson CI. 11.7's NEW work is the **execution harness that PRODUCES the per-participant fields** + the attestation gate that asserts they were **derived**. Reusing/extending is right; rewriting count reconciliation is reinvention.
- **L2 — Self-report is the trap (the whole point).** The R2 fabrication and the 10.2 canned-trap are the same failure: a value **typed into a file** and echoed as a pass. The harness MUST DERIVE `sbom_verified`/`signing_chain_verified`/`halt_recall`/`binary_loads`/`frames_run` from **real artifacts and real execution**, and must **ignore** any self-reported value that contradicts the derived one — mirror `FkcsOracle::derive`'s `ignored_self_report` (`xtask/src/check_fkcs.rs`). A harness that reads `sbom_verified` from its input and returns it is **vacuous-green**.
- **L3 — Negative controls must fail at the REAL verify path.** A tampered-SBOM Spirit must red because the **real** `cargo tree`/`Cargo.lock` closure reconciliation fails; a broken-signature Spirit must red because the **real** `maos_audit::release_verify::verify_release_signature(_, _, &RELEASE_PUBKEY)` returns error — NOT because a harness-internal `assert!` or compile error fired (11.5 literal-AC3 lesson).
- **L4 — "clean VM" hermeticity must be real and proven.** A harness that re-loads the candidate in the dev's warm environment is not a clean-VM re-load. Assert **no prior MAOS state** (no `~/.maos`, no cached candidate artifacts) and prove the assertion **reds** when a stale artifact is planted (the proven-red for AC1).
- **L5 — halt-recall must be measured, never hard-coded.** Reuse the `maos-eval` halt scoring (`HaltCorpus`/`onboarding_gate_corpus::score_candidate`) with a **SHA-256-pinned corpus** and a **`provisional` stamp** on fixture-sourced runs (`crates/maos-eval/src/onboarding_gate_corpus.rs`, `fixtures/nfr-onb-1/README.md`). - **L6 — Chinese-wall proxy honesty.** Proxies are **in-house**, isolated from the internals the negative control exploits — label them the literal string `"in-house Chinese-wall proxy"` everywhere reported; the v2.0 score is **advisory proof-of-mechanism**; the genuine-external N=12 blocking floor is **v2.5** (11.5 L7).

## Acceptance Criteria (4 — at the epic-11…md:51 count; falsifiers fold into their parent AC)

**AC1 — The hermetic clean-environment CI-bot re-load harness derives `binary_loads` + `frames_run` from real execution (NFR-Test-8 "loads on fresh Host VM, runs ≥1000 frames … re-loaded on clean VM by CI bot").**
**Given** NFR-Test-8 (`prd/non-functional-requirements.md:83`) requires each participant's Spirit to *"load on fresh Host VM, run ≥1000 frames"* and be *"re-loaded on clean VM by CI bot"*, and today `check-third-party-trial` accepts `binary_loads`/`frames_run` as **hand-authored TOML fields** (`xtask/src/check_third_party_trial.rs:38-40`);
**When** the new derivation harness (extending `maos-eval`) is handed a candidate signed Spirit artifact bundle and, in a **hermetic environment** (fresh CI runner / container; no `~/.maos`, no cached candidate artifacts — the 10.2 README §3 "clean Host VM" invariant), **re-loads** the binary through the real load path (`LocalRunner`, the `onb_1_self_trial.rs` precedent, or `maosctl install --from-local . --verify-only` per the 10.2 runbook §1) and **runs frames** against it;
**Then** `binary_loads` (kernel accepts the binary) and `frames_run` (actual frames executed) are **DERIVED from the run**, stamped with the run's environment-hermeticity provenance, and reconciled into the per-participant record — never read back from the input;
**And** the proven-reds hold: (a) a binary that **fails to load** derives `binary_loads = false` (not an error/panic that masks it); (b) a run that executes **< 1000 frames** derives `frames_run < 1000` and fails the success conjunction; (c) **planting a stale MAOS artifact** (e.g. a warm `~/.maos` cache) makes the hermeticity assertion **red** — proving "clean VM" is enforced, not assumed (L4).

**AC2 — SBOM completeness + signing-chain verification are DERIVED by the CI bot and a forged self-report is ignored (NFR-Test-8 "auditable via SBOM + signing chain").**
**Given** NFR-Test-8 requires the trial be *"auditable via SBOM + signing chain re-loaded on clean VM by CI bot"*, the 10.2 runbook (`docs/third-party-trial/sbom-signing-verification.md` §2-3) defines SBOM-completeness as *"every package in `cargo tree`/`Cargo.lock` appears in the SBOM"* and signing-chain as *"Ed25519 verify against the trusted root `RELEASE_PUBKEY`"*, and the machinery exists (`maos_audit::release_verify::{RELEASE_PUBKEY, parse_sha256sums, verify_release_signature, verify_release}`; the `check_dependency_closure.rs` `cargo tree` pattern; `deny.toml`);
**When** the harness **derives** `sbom_verified` from **TWO independent sources** (F4 tautology guard — NEVER `cargo tree` vs `cargo tree`): the candidate **declares** its dependency set by *shipping its own `Cargo.lock`* in the artifact bundle (the claim), and the harness **independently recomputes** the closure via `cargo tree --locked` over the resolved graph (the ground truth) and reconciles the declaration against it **exactly** (every package, matching name+version) plus enforces the `deny.toml` closure policy (`check_dependency_closure.rs` pattern); and **derives** `signing_chain_verified` (`SHA256SUMS` + `.sig` verify against `RELEASE_PUBKEY`, chain to trusted root, no untrusted intermediate/expired/revoked key);
**Then** both booleans are machine-derived per participant and folded into the v2.0 success conjunction (D7), and a **self-reported** `sbom_verified = true` / `signing_chain_verified = true` that **contradicts** the derived value is **IGNORED** (the derived value wins) — mirroring `FkcsOracle::derive`'s `ignored_self_report` discipline (`xtask/src/check_fkcs.rs`);
**And** the proven-reds hold at the REAL verify path (L3) — and because the v2.0 cohort is our own proxy, they use a **deliberately mismatched** control so the leg can actually red (not just green because reality lines up): (a) a proxy that **ships a `Cargo.lock` dropping/padding one transitive dep** vs the independently-recomputed `cargo tree` closure derives `sbom_verified = false`; (b) a Spirit whose `.sig` **fails Ed25519 verification** against `RELEASE_PUBKEY` (tampered `SHA256SUMS` or wrong key) derives `signing_chain_verified = false`; (c) a record that **self-reports both true** over a tampered artifact still derives **false** and fails.

**AC3 — Halt-recall ≥0.85 is MEASURED against the SHA-256-pinned corpus (not self-reported) and folded into the derive-and-reconciled per-participant conjunction (NFR-Test-8 / FR32 "recall floor ≥0.85 per Spirit class").**
