## 7. Lessons (three, each with a mechanical home)

1. **An instrument protects the axis it measures, and no other.** 16-0 gave the kernel a content hash and it worked — it caught byte-level drift on contact, twice. The kernel's *other* pin, the public-surface baseline keyed by module path, was never in scope and is red at epic close. Building a good instrument creates the belief that the surface is covered. *Home:* **E16-A1**, and the FLAG-Winston checklist obligation inside it.
2. **A forecast is not a measurement, even in a project that measures everything.** Seven stories each disproved their own epic section — and the epic's kernel forecast, the one number nobody re-derived per story until the story arrived, was out by roughly 18×. Every individual raise was honest and ratified; the aggregate was never checked against the plan until today. *Home:* **E16-A7**.
3. **A destination that accepts everything is a bucket, whatever it is called.** Rule 10 was ratified eight days ago and held perfectly at the story level — every §11 row named a vehicle. It failed one level up: the vehicle six stories named was `epic-16-retrospective`, and ~24 items arrived there because it was the only key that could not refuse them. *Home:* **E16-A3**.
