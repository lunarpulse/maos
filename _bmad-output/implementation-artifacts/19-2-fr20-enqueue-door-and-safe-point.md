> **The capability:** *the founder watches the epic run and, while Worker 2 is busy, types one more instruction into `maosctl orchestrator queue`. Nothing interrupts Worker 2. The moment it completes, the Orchestrator takes the instruction at its safe point and the next `task.assign` carries it to Worker 3 — and if the founder changes their mind first, `maosctl orchestrator recall` takes it back and it never reaches any Worker. Separately, a director's pick on a running Butler's notification finally reaches that Butler: `maosctl spirit pick butler 1` records the decision in the director's principal namespace and runs the option's action, instead of `maos shell` printing a canned string. And when the director pulls the brake, it holds: `maosctl pause <spirit>` makes every further capability-mediated action of that Spirit fail closed at once — including the hook that is mid-flight and a model call already on the wire — a paused Worker's container is frozen with its state intact, and `maosctl revoke-token` stops the Worker holding that token; both bounds are measured, not asserted.*
