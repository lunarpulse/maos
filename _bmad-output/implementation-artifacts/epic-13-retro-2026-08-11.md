## 2. What Went Well

1. **The wall became a production boundary rather than a constructed object.** The sequence from 13.1/13.2 through 13.5c, 13.5d, 13.6a, and 13.6b closed the physical, cryptographic, boot, route, identity, and emitter/applier seams. The final journey exercised them rather than assuming composition.
2. **The evidence model separated claim proof from lane enforcement.** The four ledgers bind one clean operator run and a single commit; CI correctly does not receive the operator signing key. Required live evidence can therefore be PROVEN without treating a green-but-unsigned CI lane as proof.
3. **The closer refused false equivalences.** It recorded that the host-initiated path exposes typed refusal status while the Spirit path collapses its producible causes to `Transport`; that the vetting and legal-hold crossing claims were not constructible; and that the 14-institution result is an envelope axis, not a three-team arithmetic extrapolation.
4. **The closure repaired rather than papered over its own findings.** Two-sided erase reconciliation, exact published-ledger validation, fourteen-institution isolation, and tenant-mode reachability for the `traceback` and `collective-erase` one-shots were closed before publication.
5. **The KLOC budget was made an audited premise.** The `1c9efcdd` close-out re-measured the breached ceilings, recorded the ratified formula grants and drivers, and machine-checked the result instead of treating the old ceilings as still green.

## 3. What Was Hard / Systemic

1. **Status had three competing narratives.** The Epic 13 planning header remained `in-progress`, the sprint status was the authoritative all-`done` record, and earlier DAG/dead-wire text still described predecessor gaps. Completion must be read from `sprint-status.yaml`; planning and DAG prose require a closure sweep before they are used as status evidence.
2. **“Executable” was not yet “observable.”** The journey’s demo-ability rung originally asked for an executable scene but did not ensure a watchable, signed production observation. `demo-reza` at `5ca7ce5e` closed that gap: the six production processes and the ledger-bound outputs are observable, not merely runnable.
3. **A budget declaration is not a budget close-out.** Three ceilings were red when the closer first claimed closure because `kloc-check` had not been re-run. The repeatable pattern is: measure after final repairs, identify the driver for every delta, apply only a ratified formula grant, then machine-check the premise. `1c9efcdd` is the completed instance of that pattern.
4. **“Owned by a retrospective” is a real but weak owner.** The stale-owner sweep correctly distinguished it from an open implementation owner, but it still creates one-epic slippage risk. Every such item below has a terminal disposition or a named Epic 14 preflight handoff.

## 7. Key Takeaways

1. **Authoritative status must have one source.** Use `sprint-status.yaml` for completion and make the planning header/DAG closure sweep a mandatory close-out control.
2. **A runnable path is not a demo.** The completion rung must require observable production execution and a consumable evidence artifact; `demo-reza` @ `5ca7ce5e` is the working pattern.
3. **A ceiling is not closed until it is measured after the final repair.** The `1c9efcdd` formula-grant and machine-check sequence is the reusable KLOC close-out pattern.
4. **A retrospective owner is a decision deadline, not an implementation owner.** Every such residual must be closed, explicitly risk-accepted, or handed to a named next-epic surface before the next epic starts. C2 binds all twelve handoffs to that rule.
5. **A status field nobody re-derives is not a status field.** Epic 12's B1–B6 were all *done* and all *recorded* as unfinished. §3.1 read as an optimism bug — completion claimed too early — but the same single-source defect fired in the opposite direction and hid a 6/6 result. Reconcile against the artifact, not the note; C1 makes it mechanical.
6. **A control that closes can re-open.** B2 landed at `79a880ec` and the identical merge drift re-accumulated within one epic (69 commits). Some commitments are not tasks that complete but leaks that must be plugged every close-out. Classify them that way at ratification, or they will be re-litigated every retro.
