## What this story is, in one paragraph

13.1 built the **physical** wall (database-per-team, presence-only `team_guard`). 13.2 built the **cryptographic** wall at **entry** (per-team HKDF weld; a forged same-region cross-team bundle is refused at `verify`, so it never lands). Both are **mechanism stories proven at a seam** — and at HEAD the wall they built is *inert in every bootable configuration*: `MAOS_LOOM_HOME_TEAM` is a hard boot failure (D11), and the whole replication path has zero production callers (D9).

**13.3 separates two objects the epic sketch conflated: the ROW and the CROSSING.**

- **The row is production-real in 13.3.** `source_team` genuinely persisted through `write_with_source` (closing D8 — today `apply` *discards* it), per-row attestation persisted, the read path verifying it, the clobber hazard closed, the refusal taxonomy widened. All of that lands on `store.rs`'s **actual** `read`/`scan`/`write` — the production path, not a harness — and goes live the instant team mode becomes bootable. Neither 13.1 nor 13.2 can say that.
- **The crossing is NOT production-real, and 13.3 says so.** Initiation, transport, and the consent decision need a daemon holding both a manifest and a store; **no such daemon exists** (D12), and joining them is assigned verbatim to 13.5c by ADR-055 §5. 13.3 builds the crossing **mechanism**, proves it at the verify/apply seam, and ships a **dead-wire negative that stays red until 13.5c wires it**.

It does **not** build multi-hop distillation provenance or cross-wall raw traceback (H1 → **13.3b**), collective erasure (13.5b), or multi-store routing / refusal audit / TL isolation (13.5c).

## Story

As a **MAOS operator running Reza's 400-person fintech as one governed Cortex (teams `security`, `support`, `data` — normally sharing a region)**,
I want **a cross-team share to be a real, consented, re-attested write into the other team's database — authorized by an ordered-pair `(from_team → to_team, intent)` grant in the signed org manifest so that A sharing with B never implies B may share with A, landing a row whose `source_team` and attestation are cryptographically bound and persisted, and refused with a caller-legible typed outcome that names the responsible authority when consent, freshness, or attestation fails**,
so that **cross-team collaboration exists as a governed production path rather than a test-only seam — and the three things that would otherwise silently defeat it (an unconsented reverse share, a forged foreign-team row already resident in the store, and a refusal that reaches the caller as an opaque `Transport(reason)` string) are each closed by construction and proven red.**

**H2 — where cross-team consent is decided and enforced.** The A2A router's cohort-consent chokepoint is **grep-pinned on two counts** (`maos-a2a-core/tests/cohort_consent_chokepoint_12_2.rs:12-26` — `consent_decision(` == 1, `cohort_consent_decision(` == 3), and 13.1 established that **`maos-loom-lite` and `maos-cohort` gain no dependency edge** (ADR-055 Consequences). **Recommend: a new `CrossTeamConsentPort` trait in `maos-loom-lite` (mirroring `TenantMapPort`, `tenant.rs:22-26`), implemented in `maos-bin` over `CohortManifestState`, injected at composition — consent is decided at the *crossing/apply* seam, not in the A2A router.** Rationale: the router governs *frame delivery*; this governs *row landing*. **H3 — how asymmetry is expressed in the manifest (D6 is the constraint).** `ConsentTuple` cannot carry it. **Recommend: a new `[[cross_team_consent]]` table of ordered triples `{from_team: TeamId, to_team: TeamId, intent: String}` under a new `COHORT_SCHEMA_V3`**, copying the shipped V1/V2 dispatch idiom verbatim (`manifest.rs:51-58`, `:191-254`): V1 and **V2 canonical bodies both stay byte-frozen**, V3 signs under `SIG_DOMAIN_V3` and appends the cross-team block after the teams block. Asymmetry is then **structural** — `(A,B,share)` is a distinct row from `(B,A,share)`, so the reverse share is refused by *absence*, which is the strongest possible form. Validation mirrors `validate_team_map`/`validate_consent_table` (`manifest.rs:551-648`): both teams must exist in `[[teams]]`, `from_team != to_team`, `intent` canonical (`A2AIntent::is_canonical`), duplicates rejected.

The `[u64; 2]`→`[u64; 3]` arity change is also a free compile-time forcing function that extend-V2 forfeits. *(Mary's note: a right answer with a false reason is one refactor away from being a wrong answer nobody re-checked — so the reason is recorded, not just the verdict.)* **Recommend V3.** Note `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]` is on every manifest struct and the **schema↔teams biconditional** (`manifest.rs:296-310`) must gain its V3 arm exactly (V3 ⟺ teams present; cross-team block optional-but-empty-is-not-`None`, decided at preflight).

**H4 — cross-team row identity vs the destination team's own rows (H-CLOBBER).** Three candidate closures: **(a)** add `source_team` to the UNIQUE key — changes the physical constraint, re-keys existing rows, breaks 11.2a convergence for same-team cross-region copies (**reject**); **(b)** namespace cross-team copies into a distinct `MemoryNamespace` detail so `(pid, ns_detail, key)` cannot collide with a first-party row (**recommend** — no constraint change, no re-keying, and it makes "this row came from elsewhere" visible in the namespace, consistent with `reserved_namespaces.rs` discipline); **(c)** extend the LWW `DO UPDATE` predicate to refuse when `collective_memory.source_team IS DISTINCT FROM EXCLUDED.source_team` (**recommend as belt-and-braces with (b)** — a one-clause fail-closed guard that turns a clobber into a no-op). Whatever is chosen, **a planted same-key cross-team clobber must be a proven-red test**, or this ships as a silent cross-team data-loss bug.

**H5 — what the read-time verification actually verifies (the 13.2 hand-off).** 13.2 deferred *"per-row attestation persistence (`source_team` + `merkle_root` + `region_sig` + inclusion path), the read-path verify-with-inclusion, and production seed→store wiring"* to 13.3. ## Acceptance Criteria

1. **`source_team` is persisted on the production write path — closing D8. THE ROW IS PRODUCTION-REAL; THE CROSSING IS NOT (D12).** A cross-team crossing is: build a v2 bundle under the **source** team's derived key → verify under the key derived from the **claimed** `(region, team)` (13.2, unchanged) → apply into the **destination** team's store, landing rows whose `source_team` is the **verified** source team.
   - **⚠ THE CLAIM THIS AC MAY MAKE, EXACTLY.** The *row* work lands on `store.rs`'s real `write`/`read`/`scan` and is live the moment team mode boots. The *crossing* — initiation, transport, consent decision — is a **mechanism proven at the verify/apply seam**, because no daemon holds both a manifest and a store (D12) and joining them is ADR-055 §5's verbatim assignment to **13.5c**. Do not write "production cross-team write path" anywhere; write **"the row is production-real; the crossing is not."**
   - **`write_with_source` gains a `source_team: Option<&TeamId>` parameter**; the INSERT column list **and** the `ON CONFLICT … DO UPDATE SET` clause (`store.rs:313-327`) both carry it. `apply_verified_replication_bundle` (`bundle.rs:427-438`) passes `leaf.source_team` instead of dropping it. A round-trip test proves `read_all_rows_from` returns the same `TeamId` that `verify` proved — **derived from the write codec, not re-read from the label**.
   - **Destination enforcement (currently absent):** `apply_verified_replication_bundle` canonicalizes `dest_region` and then **uses it only for a tracing log** (`bundle.rs:368-371`) — nothing checks the bundle against the destination store. Apply must **refuse** when the bundle's claimed `source_team` equals the destination `home_team` (a self-crossing is not a crossing) and when the destination store's `home_team` is not the grant's `to_team`. Fail closed, typed.
   - **⚠ BLAST RADIUS — `write_with_source` has 33 call sites across TWO crates.** `cross_region_live.rs` (**27**), `store.rs` (**2** — the `write` wrapper `:254` + the definition), `bundle.rs:428`, `tenant_wall_live.rs:284` (**this is leg #5 — it must keep asserting *served***, H6), `maos-bench/tests/t_11_2b_cross_region_slo.rs:129` (a **different crate**; `maos-bench/Cargo.toml:27` dev-deps loom-lite), and the assertion string in `read_path_chokepoint.rs`. **Strongly prefer a `WriteSource { ts, region, log_ref, team }` struct parameter, or a new `write_with_source_team(…)` that `write_with_source` delegates to** — a bare positional add is 33 mechanical edits, 27 of them in one file, with leg #5 among them. **`read_path_chokepoint.rs:105` looks the function up by the literal prefix `"pub async fn write_with_source("`** — a parameter add survives that, a **rename does not**; if you rename, update `:105` in the same commit.
   - **Cross-module access:** `apply_verified_replication_bundle` lives in `replication/bundle.rs`, and `LoomLiteStore.config` is **private** (`store.rs:54`). Use the existing `pub fn config(&self) -> &StoreConfig` at **`store.rs:656`** for the destination `home_team` check.
   - **⚠ H4 / H-CLOBBER is a ship-blocker:** the UNIQUE key omits `source_team` (`schema.rs:63`) and LWW resolves on `(source_ts, source_region)` only, so a cross-team copy can **overwrite a destination-team first-party row** with the same `(pid, namespace, key)`. Implement H4(b)+(c); a **planted same-key clobber must be proven red**.
   - **May NOT claim:** that a forget-cascade consumes `source_team` (D7 — no cascade exists; 13.5b), or that the daemon boots in tenant mode (D11 — 13.5c).

2. **Asymmetric cross-team consent: an ordered-pair grant in the signed org manifest, enforced at the crossing seam — B→A is refused when only A→B is granted.** D6 is the constraint: `ConsentTuple` names one endpoint and **cannot** express this.
   - **Manifest (H3):** `[[cross_team_consent]]` = ordered triples `{from_team, to_team, intent}` under **`COHORT_SCHEMA_V3`** / `SIG_DOMAIN_V3`, copying the V1/V2 dispatch idiom verbatim (`manifest.rs:51-58,191-254`). **V1 *and* V2 canonical bodies stay byte-frozen** — extend `v1_canonical_body_matches_frozen_golden` with a **v2 golden** before adding V3, or v2 byte-compat is unprovable afterwards. Validation mirrors `validate_team_map` (`manifest.rs:551-618`): both teams declared in `[[teams]]`, `from_team != to_team`, canonical intent, no duplicates — each a distinct typed refusal. The schema↔teams biconditional (`:296-310`) gains its V3 arm exactly.
   - **⚠ TWO SILENT FAILURES — neither is a compile error (this is the story's highest-severity trap after H4):**
     **(1) `manifest.rs:235` guards the teams block with `if self.schema_version == COHORT_SCHEMA_V2`.** A V3 manifest carries teams, but under `==` the **teams block is omitted from the canonical signed body** — the signature verifies while team→region→`datname` placement is **unsigned and substitutable**, defeating 13.1's entire manifest-authoritative premise.    - **Gate ⟺ observer by construction (12.3-P7b idiom):** the consent decision is taken at the **same** function that lands the row, so a crossing cannot be applied without a decision having been reached. The **asymmetric negative is the headline**: with only `(A→B, share)` granted, an A→B crossing lands and a B→A crossing is **refused by absence of a grant** — proven on **real cross-team writes over live Postgres**, not a hermetic stub.

3. **Per-row attestation is persisted and the read path verifies it on the TEAM axis — closing 13.2's read-time hand-off (H5).** 13.2 left the read with nothing to verify; AC1 makes the rows real, so AC3 gives the read a verifiable artifact.
   - **Additive nullable columns** (`schema.rs:76-97` `DO $$ … ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS` idiom): **`leaf_canonical_hash` (BYTEA 32)**, `merkle_root`, `region_sig`, `bundle_schema_version`, and the leaf's inclusion path — written by the apply path alongside `source_team`. First-party local rows leave them **NULL** and are unaffected (the G2 discipline: do not re-root every store).
   - **⚠ PERSIST `canonical_hash`; READ BINDS THE COMPLETE ROW — DESIGN CORRECTED 2026-07-20 (Lunarpulse-authorized, recorded in ADR-055) (settled item (a); D13).** The at-rest seal is **unpaired at HEAD** and **non-deterministic**, and apply re-seals (`db.value_data = seal(vtp(ptv(leaf.value_data)))` ≠ `leaf.value_data`), so reconstructing the canonical leaf from a DB row is impossible under a seal. Persist `CollectiveKvLeaf::canonical_hash()` (`leaf.rs:135`) at apply time; the read path verifies `verify_proof(persisted_root, persisted_leaf_hash, persisted_proof)`. **The original "bind only the exactly-round-tripping fields" design was NOT cryptographically row-binding — a tampered `value_data` row would still verify.** The implemented plaintext posture re-derives the **complete** row (including `value_data`) and compares the full canonical hash against the persisted one; apply refuses leaves whose bytes do not round-trip through the store codec so the two hashes are comparable (13.3 review). Under a configured seal the read still fails closed (D13; **13.5a** owns the unseal). Persist `bundle_schema_version` too, or `build_team_sign_payload` cannot be reconstructed.
   - **⚠ THE SIGNATURE IS THE CONTROL — the proof is convenience (settled item (b)).** Root, proof, and row all live in the **same table**, and `verify_proof` returns `true` when `siblings.is_empty() && leaf == expected_root` (`merkle.rs:192-194`) — so an attacker who can write the row sets `root := leaf_hash`, `proof := empty`, and passes. **This is the 13.2 trusted-applied-root registry in a Merkle hat — a lock with the key taped to it, third occurrence.** The actual control is verifying `region_sig` against a **composition-injected `HashMap<(Region, TeamId), [u8;32]>`** built from the shipped `derive_team_pubkey` (`sealed_export.rs:88`). Public keys only.
   - **⚠ SEAL POSTURE, STATED OUT LOUD (Decision 3).** Read-time attestation verification is defined for the **plaintext (default) posture**. Under a configured at-rest seal the collective read path is **already broken at HEAD** (D13) — `read()` returns ciphertext into `parts_to_value`. The `canonical_hash` design keeps *this AC* seal-independent, but the surrounding read path is not. **The unpaired-unseal gap is filed with 13.5a as named owner** (ADR-051 owns at-rest; 13.5a AC2 is the first story to exercise it end-to-end). It is **not** left unsuccessored — that is how the 13.2 region residual became furniture.
   - **Dedup is NOT a problem (scout-disproved).** Two distinct rows can never collide: the canonical form includes `spirit_pid`/`namespace_*`/`key`, which is exactly the table's UNIQUE key. Duplicate leaves share one hash and one proof and verify correctly. `verify_proof` never reads `leaf_index`, so dedup index drift is inert. Rebuilding the tree at apply is one line — `bundle.leaves` is in scope at `bundle.rs:373`.
   - **⚠ THIS CANNOT LIVE IN `team_guard` — verified at HEAD.** `fn team_guard(&self, spirit_pid: u32) -> Result<(), StoreError>` (`store.rs:505`) is **synchronous**, takes **only `spirit_pid`**, and runs **pre-query**: at `read` it fires at `:359`, *before* `get_client_with_timeout()` at `:360`. It has no row, no client, no `source_team`. Attestation verification is **post-query, per row** — the two sites where `region_guard` is already called (`store.rs:380` in `read`, `:449` in `scan`).
   - **Add a NEW `attestation_guard`** (`fn attestation_guard(&self, source_team: Option<&TeamId>, root: Option<&[u8]>, sig: Option<&[u8]>, proof: …) -> bool`) at those two post-query sites. **Do not overload `team_guard`** (wrong signature, wrong phase) and **do not overload `region_guard`** (its count is pinned at 2 and it is the region axis — H6). For a row with a non-NULL `source_team` it verifies (i) Merkle **inclusion** of the row's canonical leaf against the persisted root (`maos_audit::erasure::merkle::verify_proof`, `merkle.rs:183`) and (ii) the **root's signature** under the team verifying key. **A foreign-team row without valid attestation is refused, never served.**
   - **⚠ The SELECT column lists must be extended.** `read`'s query fetches only `value_kind, value_data, source_region, source_log_ref` (`store.rs:365-367`) — **no `source_team`**; `scan` is the same. Add `source_team`, `merkle_root`, `region_sig`, and the inclusion path to **both**, and shift the positional `row.get(N)` indices accordingly.
   - **⚠ `base_seed` never crosses below `CollectiveMemoryPort` (G4 still binds).** The composition root derives **public verifying keys** via the shipped `derive_team_pubkey(base_seed, region, team)` (`maos-audit/src/sealed_export.rs:88` — do **not** hand-roll it from `derive_team_signing_seed`) and injects that set; the store holds no seed. Deriving keys ≠ holding the root.

### Traps (each has already bitten this codebase, or is proven live at HEAD)
1. **Asymmetry via a `team` field on `ConsentTuple`.** One endpoint ⇒ *"anyone may send to team-b"* (D6). Ordered pair or nothing.
2. **Cross-team clobber (H4).** `source_team` is not in the UNIQUE key; LWW resolves on `(source_ts, source_region)`. A cross-team copy **overwrites** a same-key destination first-party row. Silent data loss. Prove it red.
3. **Putting attestation verification in `team_guard`.** It is **sync, `spirit_pid`-only, and pre-query** (`store.rs:505`, fires at `:359` *before* the client exists at `:360`). There is no row to verify. Use a **new post-query `attestation_guard`** at `region_guard`'s two sites — and extend the `read`/`scan` SELECT lists, which today do not even fetch `source_team` (`store.rs:365-367`).
3b. **Adding a `team_guard`/`region_guard` call site.** `read_path_chokepoint.rs` counts **source text** across three `include_str!`'d files: exactly 3 `team_guard`, exactly 2 `region_guard`, exactly 0 in `write_with_source`/`pool`. Pin the new `attestation_guard` at 2 in the same commit.
3c. **A new `CollectivePortError` variant.** Wildcard-free kernel-core match (`memory/mod.rs:189-197`), enum not `#[non_exhaustive]` ⇒ `E0004` in kernel-core ⇒ **AC6 dead**. And the ABI gates will *not* catch it — they only inspect `maos-spirit-abi` (`abi_diff.rs:8`). `cargo check -p maos-kernel-core` is the real detector.
3d. **Leaving `manifest.rs:235`'s teams-block guard as `== COHORT_SCHEMA_V2` when adding V3.** The V3 canonical body then omits teams entirely — placement becomes **unsigned and substitutable** while the signature still verifies. Silent. Not a compile error.
4. **Flipping leg #5.** It forges a `source_log_ref` — the **region** axis (11.2b D1), a different threat. G7 ratified leaving it *served*. Team-axis only (H6).
5. **`base_seed` below the port.** A signing oracle in the data tier (Vex, 13.2 Round 1). Inject **public** verifying keys only (G4).
6. **`ranged_recall` for anything cross-team.** Path-addressed, capability-free, returns everything when `spirit_filter = None`. `spirits/researcher` pins it out at compile time. Forbidden.
7. **Editing a kernel comment.** `check-kernel-baseline` counts **every line of every `.rs`** under `maos-kernel-core/src`, comments and `#[cfg(test)]` included, with **exact equality**. Even `cargo fmt` reflow reds it.
8. **Trusting the epic's file paths.** Everything is under `src/replication/`, not `src/`. `maos-tenant-map` does not exist. `ETenant*` does not exist (D10).
9. **Assuming the replication path works because tests pass.** **Zero production callers** (D9). Test-green ≠ wired.
10. **A skipped live leg is *unmeasured*, not green** (`check_cross_region_consensus.rs:127`). Provision the substrate.
11. **Claiming authorization provenance.** ADR-049 §7 forbids it in any AC, gate, or doc. Origin ≠ authorization; a consent-absent crossing is an observable state, never folded into a clean total.
12. **Writing "production cross-team write path" anywhere (D12).** It is the sentence the room struck. The row is production-real; the crossing is not. A dead-wire negative guards this claim — if you find yourself wanting to delete leg (f), the design has drifted into 13.5c.

The first design (persist `canonical_hash`, bind only the exactly-round-tripping fields) left `value_data` unbound — a tampered row would still verify.
